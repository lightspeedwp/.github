Roles may be combined under one person in smaller organisations. Always name an owner or mark as missing.

| Role | Purpose | Can be combined with |
|---|---|---|
| Governance owner | Owns AI rules, review cadence and updates. | Project sponsor, operations lead, marketing lead. |
| Content owner | Owns website accuracy, page updates and source content. | Marketing lead, editor, product owner. |
| Brand approver | Confirms tone, visual consistency and brand rules. | Content owner, marketing lead. |
| Subject matter expert | Confirms technical, product, service or sector accuracy. | Content owner in smaller teams. |
| Legal/privacy reviewer | Reviews legal, privacy, regulated or sensitive claims. | External adviser or internal compliance owner. |
| Chatbot owner | Reviews transcripts, source lists, fallback and escalation. | Support lead, operations lead, product owner. |
| Incident contact | Handles bad answers, complaints or risky outputs. | Governance owner, support lead. |
| Technical owner | Owns implementation, access, plugins, integrations and logs. | Developer, agency contact, IT lead. |

## Umbrella role suggestions

For small clients:

- Project owner: governance owner + content owner + incident contact.
- Marketing owner: content owner + brand approver.
- Operations owner: chatbot owner + escalation owner.
- External adviser: legal/privacy reviewer.
