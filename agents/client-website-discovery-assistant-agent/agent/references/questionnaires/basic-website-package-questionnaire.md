>
> Guidance: Use one accountable decision-maker where possible.

**Answer:** Name, role, email and phone number.

> Guidance: This may differ from the legal entity.

**Answer:** Use the public brand name.

### Main website goal

**Answer:** Create a credible online presence and generate enquiries.

### Primary audience

**Answer:** Prospective customers who need a quick, clear explanation of the offer.

### Main conversion action

**Answer:** Contact us / Request a quote / Book a consultation.

### Homepage hero

**Answer:** Benefit-led headline, short intro, CTA and one strong image.

>

### Contact information

**Answer:** Email, phone, location or service area, form recipient.

**Answer:** Logo, hero image, team/product/service images. Confirm usage rights.
