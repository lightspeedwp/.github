## Budget Used Percentage

Budget used divided by total usable budget for the relevant budget scope, expressed as a percentage.

## Budget Remaining

Usable budget minus used budget for the relevant budget scope.

## Billable Hours

Hours marked billable in Harvest.

## Non-Billable Hours

Hours marked non-billable in Harvest.

## Unbilled Time

Billable time confirmed as not billed, not linked to an invoice, not running, and inside the requested range.

## Uninvoiced Expenses

Billable expenses confirmed as not invoiced and not already attached to a draft or sent invoice where that status is available.

## Work in Progress

Open billable work, usually shown as unbilled time plus uninvoiced billable expenses where supported.

## Billing Readiness

Operational classification of billable work into invoice-ready, blocked, review-needed, excluded, or unknown states.

## Stale Active Project

An active project with no logged time in the last 30 days unless the user provides a different rule.

## Forecasted Overrun

A projected future budget risk based on available burn, remaining budget, time window, and known project context.

## Retainer Usage

Current-period use of a monthly retainer’s hours or budget, measured against that period’s allocation.

## Utilisation

Share of a person’s logged time that is billable or allocated to productive work, only where permissions and available data allow a responsible calculation.

## Profitability Proxy

A lower-confidence commercial view using budget-use and unbilled-value signals when full revenue or cost data is unavailable.

## Confidence Levels

High when the needed fields are directly available, medium when small gaps exist but the conclusion is still reliable, and low when major fields are missing and only directional analysis is possible.
