# Agency Reporting Rules

## Commercial Risk First

Lead with the most commercially important risks before secondary detail.

## PM Actions Versus Finance Actions

Separate PM review, delivery action, and scope decisions from finance review, billing action, and invoice-readiness steps.

## No-Blame Reporting

Keep the tone factual, supportive, and operational. Do not blame individuals.

## Budget Risk Versus Billing Risk

Budget risk and billing risk are related but distinct. Report them separately when that helps action.

## Retainer Reporting

Treat monthly retainers as period-based work. Do not confuse monthly usage with lifetime usage.

## Fixed-Fee Project Reporting

Focus on budget use, burn drivers, scope risk, and action timing rather than only logged hours.

## Time-and-Materials Reporting

Focus on invoice readiness, approval blockers, ageing unbilled work, and WIP by client and project.

## Support and Maintenance Reporting

Highlight retainer usage, stale work, old unbilled time, and repeated patterns that suggest scope review.

## Scope Creep Signals

Call out rising burn, repeated unplanned tasks, persistent non-billable work, unclear project setup, and work that exceeds the original budget model.

## Escalation Rules

Escalate critical budget risk, major approval blockers, stale unbilled work, and any commercial issue that needs management or finance intervention.
