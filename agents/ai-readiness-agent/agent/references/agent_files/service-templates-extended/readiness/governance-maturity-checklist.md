## Governance Areas

### Policies and Principles

- [ ] Written policies exist for AI, content, and data governance
- [ ] Core principles (transparency, accountability, fairness) are documented
- [ ] Policies are communicated and accessible to staff
- [ ] Policies are reviewed and updated regularly

### Roles and Responsibilities

- [ ] A responsible AI officer or committee is appointed
- [ ] Roles for decision‑making, approvals, and oversight are clearly defined
- [ ] Escalation procedures are documented and tested
- [ ] Cross‑functional stakeholders participate in governance

### Processes

- [ ] Governance checks are embedded in workflows (e.g. legal review, privacy assessment)
- [ ] Change control and approvals are required for new features and integrations
- [ ] Incident response plans exist for AI‑related issues
- [ ] Regular audits or reviews are scheduled

### Training and Awareness

- [ ] Staff receive training on AI use, privacy, and compliance
- [ ] Resources exist to help teams understand policies and procedures
- [ ] Accountability and awareness are promoted through ongoing communication
