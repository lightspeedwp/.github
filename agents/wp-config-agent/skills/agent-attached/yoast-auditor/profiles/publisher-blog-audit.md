# Publisher Blog Audit Profile

## When to load

Load for blogs, publishers, content-heavy sites or article archives.

## Audit lens

Focus on article schema, author archives, date archives, categories/tags, pagination, duplicate tags, editorial metadata consistency and sitemap coverage.

## Common risks

- Thin tag archives indexed.
- Author/date archives creating duplicate pathways.
- Missing or weak article metadata.
- Inconsistent author or organisation schema.
- Large numbers of old posts with missing descriptions.

## Output expectation

Separate technical indexation risks from editorial content judgement.
