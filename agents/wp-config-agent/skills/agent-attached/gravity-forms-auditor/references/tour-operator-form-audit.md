## Scope

Use this file for travel, safari, accommodation, itinerary, booking enquiry, quote request, and lead qualification flows.

## Audit checks

- Enquiry-first flow is clear; full booking automation is not assumed.
- Multi-page itinerary forms reduce friction and show progress.
- Destination interests are captured in a structured way.
- Travel dates support unknown/flexible dates where relevant.
- Traveller count captures adults/children/rooms only when needed.
- Accommodation preference, budget range, travel style, special interests, and mobility/accessibility needs are optional unless essential.
- Consent/privacy wording covers enquiry handling, email follow-up, and external itinerary/CRM sharing where applicable.
- File upload risk is justified; avoid passports/IDs unless secure handling is confirmed.
- Partial Entries/Save and Continue are assessed for personal data retention and spam coverage limitations.
- Deposit/payment fields are high risk and require approval, gateway/add-on evidence, and sandbox validation.
- CRM/email routing supports destination/product/team ownership.

## Retest steps

- Retest shortest and longest enquiry paths.
- Verify conditional logic for destinations/interests.
- Verify routing for representative destinations or products.
- Confirm consent and privacy copy appears before submission.
- Confirm no sensitive travel document collection unless approved.
