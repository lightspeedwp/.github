## Business basics

- Brand name: Example Travel Co.
- Primary offer: Tailor-made guided journeys
- Ideal traveler: Experience-led couples and families
- Main conversion goal: Qualified enquiries

## Site structure

- Homepage focus: Trust, clarity, and enquiry conversion
- Blog or news naming: News
- Key navigation sections: Tours, Destinations, About, Blog, Contact

## Content model

- Post types: Tours, Destinations, Accommodations, Reviews
- Taxonomies: Travel styles, destination regions, accommodation types

## Forms and SEO

- Required forms: Contact, enquiry, newsletter
- Priority landing pages: Botswana, Family Safari, Luxury Safari
