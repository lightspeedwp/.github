## Scope and timeframe

- Current open Zendesk backlog, reviewed over the last 7 days.

## Backlog health

- Backlog volume is stable overall, but high-priority ticket aging is increasing in the payments queue.
- New ticket intake is outpacing closures for one support group during the last three business days.

## Issue themes

- Repeated checkout reliability issues remain the most common pattern.
- A smaller cluster of refund-policy questions is driving avoidable handling time.

## SLA risk

- Several older high-impact tickets are approaching or exceeding expected response targets.
- Risk is concentrated in one queue rather than spread evenly across the backlog.

## Escalation-ready cases

- A small set of repeated checkout-failure cases show enough impact and repetition to justify escalation review.
- One account shows both revenue impact and repeated follow-up contacts, which raises urgency.

## Recommended next actions

- Prioritize the oldest high-impact checkout cases for immediate review.
- Rebalance ownership in the highest-risk queue.
- Group repeated checkout issues into one support pattern review.
- Review whether refund-policy macros or Help Center guidance need updating.
- Recheck backlog health after the next triage cycle.
