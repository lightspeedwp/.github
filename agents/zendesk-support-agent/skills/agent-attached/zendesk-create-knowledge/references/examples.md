Use this reference when a teammate needs a quick example of the expected article shape, when the source material is messy, or when the article type is unclear after the initial review.

All examples are deliberately anonymised. Do not copy customer names, account IDs, ticket IDs, private comments, or exact proprietary implementation details into reusable examples.

## Example 1: resolved setup question to how-to article

### Source pattern

A customer asked how to connect a supported integration. Support confirmed the correct steps and the customer confirmed the setup worked.

### Best article type

`How-to`

### Draft pattern

```md
## Knowledge Draft

**Title:** Connect the example integration
**Type:** How-to
**Category:** Integrations
**Audience:** Admin users setting up the integration
**Visibility:** Public help centre
**Tags:** integrations, setup, connection

---

This article explains how to connect the example integration from your account settings.

Use this guide when you have admin access and need to complete the first-time connection.

## Before you start

- Confirm that you have admin access.
- Confirm that your integration account is active.
- Keep the integration login details available.

## Steps

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Open **Integrations**.
3. Select **Example Integration**.
4. Choose **Connect**.
5. Follow the authorisation prompts.
6. Return to the integration screen and confirm the status is **Connected**.

## Check it worked

The integration should show a **Connected** status and the latest sync time.

## If the connection fails

Try reconnecting after confirming that the integration account is active. If the issue continues, contact support and include a screenshot of the error message.

---

### Publishing Notes
- Source: Resolved support case and customer confirmation
- Existing articles to update: None identified
- Public/internal boundary: Public steps only; no private account details included
- Evidence confidence: high — setup steps and success state were confirmed
- Review needed from: Support operations
- Suggested review date: Review if the integration setup flow changes
- Recommended next route: zendesk-evidence-quality-review before publication
```

## Example 2: repeated error message to troubleshooting article

### Source pattern

Multiple customers report the same error message. Support has confirmed the most common cause and a safe first fix.

### Best article type

`Troubleshooting`

### Draft pattern

```md
## Knowledge Draft

**Title:** Fix the "example sync failed" error
**Type:** Troubleshooting
**Category:** Sync and imports
**Audience:** Admin users troubleshooting sync errors
**Visibility:** Public help centre
**Tags:** sync, error, troubleshooting

---

The "example sync failed" error can appear when the connection token has expired or the source account is temporarily unavailable.

This article helps you check the most common confirmed causes and try the safest next steps.

## Symptoms

You may see:

- "Example sync failed"
- A sync status that remains stuck on **Failed**
- No new records imported after the last successful sync

## What to check first

1. Confirm that the source account is active.
2. Reconnect the integration from **Settings > Integrations**.
3. Run the sync again.

## If the issue continues

Contact support and include:

- the exact error message
- the time the sync failed
- whether reconnecting changed the status

---

### Publishing Notes
- Source: Repeated support cases with confirmed reconnect workaround
- Existing articles to update: Existing sync troubleshooting article may need an added branch
- Public/internal boundary: Do not include internal logs or customer account examples in the public article
- Evidence confidence: medium — pattern and workaround are supported, but article overlap should be checked
- Review needed from: Support operations
- Suggested review date: Review after the next integration release
- Recommended next route: zendesk-evidence-quality-review
```

## Example 3: product limitation to FAQ

### Source pattern

Customers repeatedly ask whether a feature supports a specific workflow. Support has confirmed that the workflow is not currently supported.

### Best article type

`FAQ`

### Draft pattern

```md
## Knowledge Draft

**Title:** Can I use example feature with multiple workspaces?
**Type:** FAQ
**Category:** Account settings
**Audience:** Customers planning workspace setup
**Visibility:** Public help centre
**Tags:** workspace, feature limit, account settings

---

No. Example feature currently works with one workspace at a time.

## Details

If you manage multiple workspaces, choose the workspace that should use the feature first. You can change the connected workspace later, but the feature will not run across multiple workspaces at the same time.

## Need help?

Contact support if you are unsure which workspace should be connected.

---

### Publishing Notes
- Source: Confirmed support answer and product limitation note
- Existing articles to update: Account setup FAQ may need this as an added question
- Public/internal boundary: Avoid roadmap promises or future timeline language
- Evidence confidence: high — limitation is confirmed and wording avoids unsupported timelines
- Review needed from: Product support
- Suggested review date: Review if multi-workspace support is released
- Recommended next route: zendesk-evidence-quality-review if publishing publicly
```

## Example 4: temporary workaround to known issue article

### Source pattern

A known issue affects a specific workflow. Support has a temporary workaround, but the permanent fix is not yet confirmed.

### Best article type

`Known issue`

### Draft pattern

```md
## Knowledge Draft

**Title:** Known issue: example reports may not refresh after changing filters
**Type:** Known issue
**Category:** Reporting
**Audience:** Customers using saved reports
**Visibility:** Public help centre
**Tags:** reports, filters, known issue, workaround

---

Some saved reports may not refresh after filters are changed. A workaround is available while this issue is under review.

## Current status

Support has confirmed the issue for saved reports using custom filters. Do not include a fix date unless one has been approved for public communication.

## Who is affected

This may affect customers using saved reports with custom filters.

## Workaround

1. Open the saved report.
2. Remove the custom filter.
3. Save the report.
4. Reapply the filter.
5. Refresh the report.

## What support needs

If the workaround does not help, contact support with:

- the report name
- the filters used
- the time the report last refreshed successfully

---

### Publishing Notes
- Source: Confirmed support cases and internal known-issue note
- Existing articles to update: Reporting troubleshooting article may need a link to this known issue
- Public/internal boundary: Public article can include symptoms and workaround; internal investigation details must stay out
- Evidence confidence: medium — issue and workaround are confirmed, but product status should be reviewed before publishing
- Review needed from: Product support
- Suggested review date: Review weekly until status changes
- Recommended next route: zendesk-evidence-quality-review
```

## Example 5: support-only diagnostic guidance to internal note

### Source pattern

Support needs repeatable diagnostic guidance for a sensitive or account-specific issue that should not be published publicly.

### Best article type

`Internal note`

### Draft pattern

```md
## Knowledge Draft

**Title:** Internal note: diagnosing example billing sync mismatches
**Type:** Internal note
**Category:** Billing support
**Audience:** Support agents
**Visibility:** Internal support knowledge
**Tags:** billing, sync, internal, diagnostics

---

> Internal support knowledge only. Do not publish this article publicly without review.

## When to use this note

Use this note when a customer reports that billing records do not match the latest sync result.

## Confirmed symptoms or conditions

- Customer sees different totals between billing and sync records.
- The sync completed successfully.
- The mismatch appears after a recent account configuration change.

## Diagnostic steps

1. Check the latest sync timestamp.
2. Confirm whether account settings changed before the mismatch appeared.
3. Compare the affected record IDs in the internal support view.
4. Escalate if the mismatch affects billing totals or customer invoices.

## Safe customer-facing wording

We are checking the latest sync details for your account and will confirm the next safest step once we have reviewed the affected records.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate when:

- billing totals are affected
- customer invoices may be incorrect
- the mismatch repeats after a successful resync
