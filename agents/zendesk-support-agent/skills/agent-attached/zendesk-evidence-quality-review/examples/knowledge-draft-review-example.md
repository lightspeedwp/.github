## Draft to review

> If your product cards look broken, disable your custom theme override. This fixes the issue.

## Correct review output

## Verdict

Internal-only for now. The workaround may be useful for support investigation, but the supplied evidence does not support public help-centre guidance.

## Reusability judgement

The issue and workaround are confirmed only for one customer. The cause, scope, and wider applicability need verification before publication.

## Issues to fix

### Stability of guidance

- "This fixes the issue" is too broad. It is only confirmed for one case.

### Scope and exclusions

- Add scope: custom theme override, affected template, environment, product version, and whether caching was involved.

### Public versus internal boundary

- Keep this as an internal troubleshooting note until confirmed across more than one environment or approved by the product/support owner.

## Recommended edits

> Internal note: In one case, disabling a custom theme override resolved broken product card display. This is not yet confirmed as a general fix. Before sharing publicly, verify affected versions, theme conditions, caching state, and whether the issue reproduces without the custom override.
