Use this as the default structure when the user asks for a general QA review.

## Verdict

State whether the draft is ready, mostly ready with fixes, or not ready. Keep this to one or two sentences.

## What is strong

- Identify two to five useful strengths already present in the draft.

## Issues to fix

### Missing evidence

- Claim or section:
- Why it needs support:
- Minimum evidence needed:

### Unsupported or overstated claims

- Original wording:
- Risk:
- Safer replacement:

### Weak next steps

- Current next step:
- Why it is weak:
- Stronger next step:

### Risk or handoff gaps

- Gap:
- Why it matters:
- Recommended fix:

## Recommended edits

Provide concise rewrite suggestions or replacement lines. Do not add new facts unless they are already present in the supplied material.

## Quick pass/fail checklist

- Evidence supports the main claims: Yes/No
- Facts and interpretation are clearly separated: Yes/No
- Next steps are specific and owned: Yes/No
- Risk or escalation gaps remain: Yes/No
- Safe to send or share as-is: Yes/No
