# Tone Playbook

Use a calm, professional, and direct support voice. Be warm without sounding casual about impact.

## Frustrated Customers

Do:

- acknowledge the impact early
- show continuity by referencing what has already happened
- avoid defensive explanations
- give the clearest safe next step

Avoid:

- "We understand your frustration" as a generic opener
- vague reassurance without action
- blaming a third party unless directly relevant and confirmed

## Apology or Recovery

Use apologies for impact or handling issues, not for unconfirmed fault.

Better:

- "I am sorry this has taken longer than expected."
- "I can see this has blocked your team from moving forward."
- "We should have been clearer in our previous update."

Avoid:

- over-apologising across multiple paragraphs
- promising compensation or future prevention unless approved

## Limitation or Decline

Be direct and useful.

Better:

- "That option is not available in the current setup."
- "The safest workaround is..."
- "I do not want to promise that this can be changed until we have reviewed..."

Avoid:

- fake optimism
- "hopefully"
- implying the product roadmap will change

## Technical Customers

Use precise wording, but do not overload the reply. Include steps, constraints, affected areas, and validation details only when they help the customer act.
