# Localisation Rules

Use these rules when translating or localising a support reply.

## Preserve Support Intent

Translate the accountability, factual limits, and next steps. Do not translate mechanically if the result sounds unnatural.

## Keep Product Terms Stable

Keep product, plan, plugin, company, ticket, and feature names unchanged unless the source material confirms a localised name.

## Dates and Times

Use unambiguous dates and times. Include timezone information when timing matters.

## Formality

Match the requested locale and customer relationship. When unsure, prefer professional formality over casual phrasing.

## Do Not Add Facts

Do not add local legal, billing, security, or product details during localisation unless they are present in the source evidence.
