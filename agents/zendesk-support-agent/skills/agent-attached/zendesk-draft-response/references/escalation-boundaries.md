Route away from reply drafting or keep the reply deliberately limited when the issue involves:

- confirmed or likely security/data exposure
- billing dispute, refund demand, credit request, or chargeback risk
- executive complaint or high-value account risk
- repeated missed commitments
- possible outage, incident, or broad customer impact
- legal, compliance, privacy, or contractual interpretation
- product roadmap decision or feature commitment
- engineering ownership that has not been confirmed

A safe customer reply can still acknowledge receipt and set a limited next step, but it must not promise the escalation outcome.
