## Overall support picture

- The week shows higher created volume than solved volume, so the open backlog increased.
- The main risk is ageing open work where ownership is unclear.
- Repeated checkout and onboarding questions are the best candidates for duplicate review or macro refinement.

## Highest-priority risks

| Risk | Evidence | Impact | Recommended action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ageing high-priority checkout cases | 2 high-priority checkout tickets are older than 5 days | Customer-impacting workflow and likely escalation risk | Assign a single owner and confirm the next customer-facing update |
| Onboarding confusion | 5 tickets share onboarding setup terms | Repeated support pain | Review the macro and Help Centre article for missing setup steps |

## Recommended support actions

1. Prioritise high-priority checkout cases older than 5 days.
2. Review unassigned open tickets before lower-risk pending tickets.
3. Group onboarding setup tickets and identify whether a macro update would reduce repeat questions.
4. Mark SLA as unavailable in stakeholder updates unless SLA data becomes visible.
