Use this reference before finalising a backlog, trend, weekly, daily, or repeated-theme report.

## Evidence checks

- Counts are based on visible Zendesk evidence, not estimates.
- Date windows and filters are stated.
- Current and comparison windows use equivalent filters when trend claims are made.
- SLA, CSAT, Help Centre, and custom-field availability are stated when relevant.
- Ticket examples are representative and necessary, not excessive.
- Missing evidence is clearly labelled.

## Interpretation checks

- Root cause is not claimed unless confirmed in Zendesk or a cited secondary source.
- Incident wording is cautious unless incident evidence is confirmed.
- Duplicate wording distinguishes likely duplicate, related but distinct, repeated support pain, possible incident signal, and inconclusive.
- Risk labels explain why the risk matters.

## Action checks

- Recommended actions are support-owned by default.
- Each action has a concrete verb and target.
- Downstream systems are not introduced unless requested or supported by evidence.
- Write actions are not performed unless explicitly requested and available.

## Shared-agent checks

- No personal login, private view, local path, or user-specific memory is required.
- No hardcoded Zendesk IDs are used unless provided by the active request or environment.
- Permission limitations are framed as unavailable evidence, not as negative findings.

## Output checks

- No unresolved placeholders, TODOs, or TBDs remain.
- Empty sections are removed.
- The report is concise enough for support leads to act on quickly.
- Evidence basis and gaps are included.
