## Warm leads after a resource download

- role: educational nurture
- primary CTA: book a consultation or request a review
- fallback: reply with a question or explore a service page
- main friction: unclear fit and unclear next step

## Launch or update email

- role: announcement with action
- primary CTA: explore the launch or offer
- fallback: read supporting detail
- main friction: relevance and urgency

## Newsletter for existing contacts

- role: nurture or reactivation
- primary CTA: continue the relationship or take the next focused action
- fallback: read, reply, or explore supporting content
- main friction: competing priorities
