# Minimum Inputs Before Generating a Governance Guide

Do not produce a final governance guide without enough information to complete these areas. If missing, ask focused questions.

## Minimum required inputs

| Input | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Client name and website/domain | Identifies the scope of the governance document. |
| Business model and sector | Determines risk, content types and AI use-case boundaries. |
| Locations/regions served | Flags privacy, localisation and regulatory considerations. |
| Approved AI use cases | Defines what AI may support. |
| Excluded AI use cases | Prevents scope creep and risky automation. |
| Sensitive data context | Determines privacy and handling controls. |
| Content owner | Defines who approves website copy and source updates. |
| Governance owner | Defines who maintains rules and review cycles. |
| Source materials | Identifies what AI may use for drafting or chatbot grounding. |
| Chatbot intent, if relevant | Defines chatbot scope, boundaries and escalation. |

## Minimum follow-up questions

Ask these if details are missing:

1. What should AI help with first?
2. What should AI never do for this business?
3. What data or information must never be entered into AI tools?
4. Who approves AI-assisted content before publication?
5. Which source documents are approved and current?
6. Is the sector regulated or reputationally sensitive?
7. Will any AI output be public-facing?
8. Will the website or chatbot collect personal information?
9. Who handles errors, complaints or unsafe answers?
10. How often should the governance rules be reviewed?
