Use these line items when turning AI readiness outputs into LightSpeed proposal notes. Keep wording practical, scoped and evidence-aware.

## Discovery and strategy

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| AI Website Readiness Assessment | Review website, content, governance and chatbot readiness. | Readiness report and priority roadmap. |
| AI Governance Discovery Workshop | Gather business, content, data, risk and approval context. | Discovery summary and governance inputs. |
| Content and Source Audit | Review existing pages, documents and source quality. | Gap report and source-of-truth register. |
| AI Readiness Roadmap | Sequence foundation, governance, content, chatbot and launch work. | Phased roadmap with assumptions and exclusions. |

## Foundation work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Website Technical Audit | Identify performance, accessibility, SEO and maintainability issues. | Audit report and remediation backlog. |
| Content Structure and Sitemap Review | Improve page purpose, internal linking and structured content. | Sitemap, page model and content priorities. |
| WordPress Pattern / Template Review | Assess reusable blocks, patterns, theme settings and editor workflow. | Design system and build recommendations. |
| Analytics and Conversion Baseline | Confirm measurement for enquiries, forms, downloads and chatbot handoffs. | GA4/GTM event plan or baseline notes. |

## Governance work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| AI Governance Guide | Define approved AI use, exclusions, roles, review and risk controls. | Operational governance guide. |
| AI Content Policy | Create a lightweight policy for internal content teams. | Policy document or review checklist. |
| Prompt and Review Workflow Setup | Create approved prompts and review routes. | Prompt templates and workflow rules. |
| AI Disclosure / Trust Content | Explain AI-assisted content or chatbot use in plain language. | Review-safe page wording or disclosure notes. |

## Content and source work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Content Collection Sprint | Gather source material and approvals. | Content tracker and folder pack. |
| Page Brief Creation | Create reusable briefs for priority pages. | Page briefs for homepage, services, products, FAQs and case studies. |
| FAQ and Knowledge Base Development | Build AI-safe source content. | Approved FAQs and source documents. |
| Claim Register Creation | Validate claims, statistics and outcomes before publication or chatbot use. | Claim register with approved/rejected/needs-evidence statuses. |

## Chatbot work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Chatbot Readiness Review | Decide whether a chatbot should proceed. | Go/no-go brief. |
| Chatbot Behaviour Guide | Define scope, tone, sources, fallback and escalation. | Chatbot operating guide. |
| Chatbot Prototype Planning | Prepare platform, source and testing requirements. | Implementation brief and launch gate. |
| Chatbot Test Script and Launch Gate | Validate intents, refusals, escalation and source grounding. | Test matrix and go/no-go notes. |

## Discoverability and launch work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Schema and AI Discoverability Planning | Prepare page-level schema, FAQs, internal linking and answer-engine notes. | Schema and AI-search recommendation plan. |
| Launch Readiness QA | Validate forms, accessibility, analytics, redirects, SEO and chatbot handoff. | Launch checklist and blocker list. |
| Post-launch Optimisation Cycle | Review analytics, search, chatbot logs and content gaps. | Monthly or quarterly improvement backlog. |

## Retainer work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Governance Review | Review AI outputs, chatbot logs and source updates. | Monthly review notes and action list. |
| Content Performance Review | Review analytics, search, FAQs and content gaps. | Monthly or quarterly improvement plan. |
| Chatbot Improvement Cycle | Review transcripts, failures and new intents. | Updated tests, sources and prompt rules. |

## Proposal note rules

- Include assumptions, exclusions and required client inputs beside any estimate recommendation.
- Avoid promising chatbot implementation until source, governance, privacy and escalation gates are passed.
- Mark legal/privacy/regulatory items as requiring qualified review.
- For thin evidence, recommend a discovery or readiness phase rather than a fixed implementation quote.
