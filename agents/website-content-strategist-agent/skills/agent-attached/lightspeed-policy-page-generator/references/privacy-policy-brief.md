A Privacy Policy brief should define what the final policy needs to cover, not pretend to be final legal wording.

## Cover

- organisation / website owner
- contact details for privacy questions
- types of personal data collected
- how data is collected
- why data is collected
- where data is stored or sent
- third-party processors/tools
- analytics and tracking
- forms and lead capture
- newsletter or marketing consent
- chatbot transcript handling, if applicable
- retention periods or retention decision notes
- user rights and request routes
- security and access controls
- international transfer considerations, if applicable
- update/review date

## WordPress notes

Check:

- forms and form plugins
- analytics/GTM/GA4
- CRM/email integrations
- spam protection tools
- cookie/consent plugin
- chatbot or AI plugin
- hosting/logging setup
- backups and retention

## Output stance

Mark as `Legal/Privacy Review Required` until reviewed.
