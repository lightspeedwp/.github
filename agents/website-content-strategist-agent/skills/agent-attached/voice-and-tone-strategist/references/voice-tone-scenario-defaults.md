# Voice and tone scenario defaults

## Standard brochure site

Recommended starting point:

- Practical and credible
- Low-pressure consultative
- Clear and confidence-building

## WooCommerce store

Recommended starting point:

- Clear and benefit-led
- Commercial but not hype-heavy
- Trust-building around purchase confidence

## Tour operator site

Recommended starting point:

- Premium and reassuring
- Expert-led and trust-aware
- Avoid exaggerated travel cliches and unsupported promises

## Policy or trust page

Recommended starting point:

- Clear and restrained
- High-trust and low-drama
- Precision over persuasion

## Newsletter flow

Recommended starting point:

- Audience-led and purposeful
- Slightly lighter than core site copy when appropriate
- CTA clarity without sounding pushy

## Forms and responder workflow

Recommended starting point:

- Reassuring and clear
- Action-led but calm
- Trust and expectation-setting first
