## Audience options

- first-time high-intent buyer
- comparison-stage evaluator
- returning customer or client
- stakeholder researching fit
- existing contact needing the next step

## User intent options

- understand whether this is relevant
- compare options
- build trust before action
- get clarity on process or pricing
- take a direct next step now

## Primary conversion goal options

- book a consultation
- request a quote or proposal
- submit an enquiry
- start a signup or purchase flow
- access a resource or lead magnet

## Fallback action options

- explore services
- review FAQs
- read a case study
- download a resource
- contact support or sales

## Friction options

- lack of trust
- unclear offer fit
- unclear process
- unclear pricing or commitment
- missing proof
