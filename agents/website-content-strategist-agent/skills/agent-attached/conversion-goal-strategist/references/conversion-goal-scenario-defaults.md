## Standard brochure site

Recommended starting point:

- audience: comparison-stage evaluator
- primary conversion: consultation or enquiry
- fallback action: explore services or FAQs
- main friction: trust and fit clarity

## WooCommerce site

Recommended starting point:

- audience: high-intent buyer
- primary conversion: add to cart or start purchase
- fallback action: explore products or FAQs
- main friction: trust, clarity, and purchase confidence

## Tour operator site

Recommended starting point:

- audience: high-intent trip planner
- primary conversion: itinerary enquiry or consultation
- fallback action: explore destinations or FAQs
- main friction: trust, decision confidence, and proof of fit

## Policy or trust page

Recommended starting point:

- audience: trust-checking visitor
- primary conversion: reassurance and route continuation
- fallback action: contact or policy-hub navigation
- main friction: confidence and clarity

## Newsletter flow

Recommended starting point:

- audience: existing contact or warm prospect
- primary conversion: click-through to the target action
- fallback action: continue reading or explore the site
- main friction: relevance and clarity of value

## Forms and responder workflow

Recommended starting point:

- audience: action-ready visitor
- primary conversion: complete the form correctly
- fallback action: seek clarification or alternate contact route
- main friction: uncertainty about what happens next
