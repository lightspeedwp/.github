**Applies to:** Publisher Website Package, Tour Operator Website Package, WooCommerce Website Package, WordPress Foundations Package, Custom Website Solution

## Purpose

Provide post-launch maintenance, optimisation, and ongoing support after the website project is complete.

## Use when

- the client wants ongoing support after launch
- the website will evolve and needs regular maintenance or optimisation

## Includes

- agreed maintenance and review cadence
- update and support scope within plan
- prioritised optimisation recommendations
- recurring reporting or review summary where relevant

## Excludes

- unlimited redesign work
- major new features outside plan scope
- deep strategic consulting unless separately agreed

## Onboarding questions

- Do you want technical maintenance only or optimisation support too?
- What review cadence do you want?
- Who will be the ongoing point of contact?
