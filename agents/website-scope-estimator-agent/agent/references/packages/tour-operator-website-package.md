**Commercial model:** Fixed fee  
**Prerequisite:** Audit first

## Purpose

Deliver or refresh a WordPress website for tour operators, travel brands, and experience-led businesses with structured journeys, package discovery, and booking-oriented conversion paths.

## Use when

- the business sells tours, itineraries, activities, or travel packages
- package comparison, destinations, departures, and booking journeys matter

## Includes

- audit and discovery
- information architecture for tours, destinations, and package types
- priority templates for listings and detail pages
- conversion-path planning for enquiry or booking
- content-structure recommendations
- analytics baseline

## Excludes

- bespoke booking engine development
- complex itinerary system development
- enterprise travel platform integrations beyond standard scope

## Fixed-fee eligibility

Remains fixed-fee when:

- package and booking complexity stay within standard website scope
- the booking flow does not require major custom engineering

## Onboarding questions

- What kinds of tours or experiences are sold?
- Is booking handled on-site or through a third-party platform?
- Are departures, destinations, accommodation, and itineraries structured consistently?
- Is this mainly brochure-led, enquiry-led, or booking-led?

## Values the agent should gather

- `client_integrations_summary`
- `client_page_count_estimate`
- `client_has_search`
- `client_content_migration_need`
