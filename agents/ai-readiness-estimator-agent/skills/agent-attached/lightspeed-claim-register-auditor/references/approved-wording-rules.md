## Avoid

- guaranteed results
- fully compliant
- 100% accurate
- zero risk
- always
- never, unless literally true and evidenced
- best-in-class without benchmark
- AI-ready without qualification
- proven ROI without source

## Prefer

- designed to support
- can help improve
- measured through
- based on approved source material
- typical target
- pilot target
- subject to evidence and review
- where the implementation supports it

## Rewrite examples

| Risky wording | Safer wording |
|---|---|
| Guaranteed AI citations | Improves the structured signals that AI search systems can interpret and cite |
| Fully GDPR compliant | Designed to support GDPR-aware workflows, subject to legal/privacy review |
| 100% brand accuracy | Human-reviewed output aligned to approved brand and source material |
| 89% chatbot resolution rate | Pilot success metric to measure once approved source content and escalation rules are in place |
| 400% more featured snippets | Improves eligibility for answer-led search experiences when content and schema are approved |
