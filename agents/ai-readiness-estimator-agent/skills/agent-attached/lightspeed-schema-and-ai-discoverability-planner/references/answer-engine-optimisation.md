## Practical principles

- Use clear entity names consistently.
- Write answer-first summaries near the top of pages.
- Use visible FAQs where they genuinely answer user questions.
- Add tables, definitions and short summaries where useful.
- Link services, solutions, case studies and FAQs together logically.
- Keep claims evidence-backed and easy to verify.
- Add author, organisation and review context where relevant.
- Keep policy and governance pages easy to find.

## Avoid

- claiming guaranteed AI citations
- overusing AI buzzwords
- generating thin FAQ content for schema only
- hiding content from users but exposing it to machines
- publishing unverified ROI, traffic or accuracy numbers
