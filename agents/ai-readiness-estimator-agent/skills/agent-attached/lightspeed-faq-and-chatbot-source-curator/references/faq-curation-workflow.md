## 1. Collect and classify

Collect all supplied questions and classify by:

- source page
- audience
- topic
- intent
- funnel stage
- approval status
- claim risk
- chatbot suitability
- schema suitability

## 2. Group by topic

Default groups:

1. About LightSpeed
2. WordPress services
3. WooCommerce services
4. Design systems and development
5. Hosting, support and maintenance
6. Publishing and content operations
7. Tourism and specialist sectors
8. AI readiness and governance
9. Pricing and consultation
10. Policies, privacy and accessibility

## 3. Deduplicate

Merge questions when they ask the same thing with minor wording differences.

Keep the version that is:

- clearest for users
- broad enough for the company FAQ
- specific enough for page-level FAQ if needed
- safe for schema
- safe for chatbot if approved

## 4. Rewrite for answer-first clarity

Good FAQ answers:

- answer directly in the first sentence
- avoid sales waffle
- link to the next useful page or action
- avoid unsupported claims
- avoid legal/privacy/compliance advice unless approved policy wording exists
- use UK English

## 5. Map to pages

Every FAQ should have:

- primary source page
- optional related pages
- owner/reviewer
- status
- schema suitability
- chatbot-safe status

## 6. Produce launch notes

Flag:

- missing answers
- weak answers
- unsupported questions
- escalation questions
- policy/legal review items
- duplicate questions
- source pages that need stronger content
