## Chatbot-safe criteria

Mark a source or FAQ as `Chatbot Safe` only if it is:

- approved for public use
- current
- visible on the public site or approved for the chatbot knowledge base
- owned by a reviewer or team
- free from unverified claims
- free from confidential or internal-only context
- safe to quote or paraphrase
- not legal, privacy, compliance, security or urgent support advice unless it uses approved policy wording

## Status labels

Use:

- Chatbot Safe
- Chatbot Safe After Review
- Not for Chatbot
- Legal Review Required
- Evidence Required
- Escalate to Human

## Always exclude

- raw internal stats
- draft strategy notes
- private client information
- unpublished claims
- unsupported ROI or AI performance claims
- security instructions
- legal/privacy/compliance advice
- pricing promises or SLA commitments unless approved

## Safe fallback

When a question cannot be answered safely:

> I do not have an approved source for that answer yet. The best next step is to speak to the LightSpeed team so they can review your project properly.
