A Cookie Policy brief should describe what cookie/tracking information must be collected before final wording is drafted.

## Required decisions

- Which cookies are essential?
- Is GA4 installed?
- Is GTM installed?
- Are advertising or remarketing tags used?
- Are embedded videos, maps or third-party widgets used?
- Is a chatbot used?
- Does the site use A/B testing, heatmaps or session recordings?
- What consent plugin/tool is used?
- Can users change preferences?

## Categories

Use plain categories:

- strictly necessary
- analytics
- marketing
- preferences
- embedded/third-party content
