The AI Governance page is public trust content. It should explain how LightSpeed uses AI responsibly without overclaiming legal or technical certainty.

## Cover

- human accountability
- quality review
- transparency and disclosure
- privacy and data protection principles
- source control and approved content
- licensing and open-source compatibility
- bias and inclusivity checks
- tool vetting
- prompt/output documentation, if used
- incident or correction route
- review cycle

## Avoid

- claiming AI is risk-free
- promising perfect accuracy
- saying data is never processed by third parties unless confirmed
- legal compliance guarantees

## Useful structure

1. Our approach to AI
2. Human review and accountability
3. Transparency and disclosure
4. Privacy and client data
5. Open-source and licensing
6. Quality, bias and source control
7. How we review and improve AI use
8. Contact / questions
