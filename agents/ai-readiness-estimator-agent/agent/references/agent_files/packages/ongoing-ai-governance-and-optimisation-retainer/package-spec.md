# Ongoing AI Governance and Optimisation Retainer

## Package Name

Ongoing AI Governance and Optimisation Retainer

## Purpose

Provide a recurring support layer after launch to keep AI content, chatbot behaviour, and technical visibility in good condition.

## Use When

- the client has completed implementation
- ongoing review, tuning, and reporting are needed
- the main need is recurring governance and optimisation rather than a new project package

## Includes

- monthly or quarterly review cycle
- website health and AI visibility review
- chatbot transcript or behaviour review where relevant
- governance and source-content review
- prompt and behaviour tuning within agreed limits
- FAQ and content improvement recommendations
- review summary and next-step actions

## Excludes

- large rebuilds
- major new feature development
- new integrations
- full content-production retainer

## Onboarding Questions

- Do you already have an AI chatbot or AI content workflow live?
- What review cadence do you want?
- What are the main optimisation priorities?
- Who will own internal approvals and actions?

## Values The Agent Should Gather From The Live Site Or References

- GA4 status
- FAQ and source-content presence
- internal owner
- sensitive or regulated use cases
- existing chatbot or AI workflow status
- optimisation priorities from prior project materials

## What The Agent Should Ask The Client To Confirm

- review cadence
- optimisation priorities
- internal owner
- whether the retainer should cover chatbot behaviour, website AI visibility, or both

## Fixed-Fee Eligibility Rules

Remains fixed-fee when:

- support stays within the standard review cadence
- no major rebuild or redesign work is requested inside the retainer
- the work remains optimisation, governance, and reporting rather than project-scale delivery

## Custom-Scope Triggers

- large rebuilds or redesigns
- major new features or integrations
- substantial content-production requirements
- unresolved launch debt that should be handled as project work first

## Likely Related Add-Ons

- none by default; attach only after the base implementation path already exists and the retainer is the right next step

## Next-Step / Routing Notes

- Use only after implementation or launch work is already live.
- Confirm cadence and optimisation priorities before quoting.
- Move project-scale delivery items out of the retainer and into separate scope.
