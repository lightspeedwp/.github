## Package Name

Yoast AI Content Training

## Purpose

Enable the client team to use Yoast and related SEO/AI content practices more effectively after implementation.

## Use When

- the client team manages content internally
- they need editorial enablement
- the site needs better habits around content clarity, metadata, and internal linking
- implementation exists or is being delivered alongside training

## Includes

- training preparation
- Yoast feature walkthrough
- metadata and content clarity guidance
- internal-linking and content-structure guidance
- AI-readiness editorial best practices
- Q&A session
- editorial checklist or training notes

## Excludes

- full content strategy
- copywriting delivery
- large-scale implementation during training

## Onboarding Questions

- Who will attend the training?
- What content tasks does the team handle internally?
- What are the biggest content workflow pain points?
- Is Yoast already in use?

## Values The Agent Should Gather From The Live Site Or References

- platform
- blog and FAQ content presence
- Site Kit status where relevant
- current Yoast usage
- editorial ownership and internal owner
- primary content goals and workflow pain points from references

## What The Agent Should Ask The Client To Confirm

- attendee roles
- current Yoast maturity
- key editorial pain points
- whether the team wants enablement only or follow-on optimisation support

## Fixed-Fee Eligibility Rules

Remains fixed-fee when:

- delivery stays within a standard workshop or training-session format
- no multi-team custom programme is required
- the work remains enablement rather than implementation delivery

## Custom-Scope Triggers

- multi-team or multi-brand training programmes
- requests for full content strategy or copy delivery
- large implementation tasks requested during training
- bespoke editorial operations design beyond standard enablement

## Likely Related Add-Ons

- none by default; pair with a base package or retainer only after the primary package path is set

## Next-Step / Routing Notes

- Use this after or alongside implementation where the client team will maintain content internally.
- Confirm attendee roles and expected outcomes before quoting.
- Route to the retainer if ongoing review and reinforcement are needed after training.
