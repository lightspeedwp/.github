# AI Chatbot with AI Engine — Starter

## Package Name

AI Chatbot with AI Engine — Starter

## Purpose

Launch a simple, well-bounded chatbot that answers common questions and routes users to the correct next step.

## Use When

- the client needs a general website assistant
- the bot will answer FAQs and service questions
- the bot does not require complex lead qualification or integrations
- the chatbot brief is already approved

## Includes

- audit and readiness check
- AI Engine configuration for the approved use case
- bot naming, greeting, labels, and disclosure text
- approved source-content setup
- FAQ and service-answer behaviour
- contact or enquiry routing
- fallback wording
- basic QA and launch support
- one revision round

## Excludes

- CRM integration
- product-aware flows
- complex booking workflows
- multilingual chatbot design
- ongoing optimisation after launch

## Onboarding Questions

- What source pages should the chatbot use?
- What should happen when the bot cannot answer?
- Where should enquiries be routed?
- Should the bot identify itself as AI?
- Is any user data being captured?

## Values The Agent Should Gather From The Live Site Or References

- website URL
- approved FAQ or service-content pages
- site-search presence
- sensitive or regulated use cases
- integrations summary
- existing contact or enquiry routes
- disclosure or policy-page presence

## What The Agent Should Ask The Client To Confirm

- approved source pages
- contact route
- disclosure wording preference
- escalation route
- whether the use case remains within a simple FAQ or service-routing scope

## Fixed-Fee Eligibility Rules

Remains fixed-fee when:

- the use case is simple FAQ, service, or enquiry routing
- approved source content already exists
- no advanced workflow logic or integrations are required
- the build stays within standard Starter capability

## Custom-Scope Triggers

- CRM or other deep integrations
- product-aware logic
- complex booking or workflow logic
- multilingual chatbot design
- requests that extend into ongoing optimisation or tailored qualification flows

## Likely Related Add-Ons

- none by default; route to Tailored instead of forcing add-ons if the use case exceeds Starter scope

## Next-Step / Routing Notes

- Use only after the planning workshop or equivalent definition work is complete.
- Confirm source pages and escalation before quoting.
- Escalate to Tailored when the use case needs structured qualification, richer routing, or more complex logic.
