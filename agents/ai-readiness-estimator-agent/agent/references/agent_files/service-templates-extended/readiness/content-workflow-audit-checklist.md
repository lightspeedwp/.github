# Content and Workflow Audit Checklist

Use this checklist to evaluate the current state of content assets and workflows as part of an AI readiness assessment.

## Client

- **Client:** {{client.name}}
- **Website:** {{client.website_url}}

## Audit Areas

### Content Inventory

- [ ] Main content types (e.g. product pages, FAQs, blog posts) are catalogued
- [ ] Ownership and maintenance responsibilities are documented
- [ ] Outdated content is identified
- [ ] Unsupported claims or pricing information are flagged
- [ ] Content gaps affecting user needs are recorded

### Workflow Process

- [ ] Steps from content creation to publication are documented
- [ ] Approval checkpoints are clearly defined
- [ ] Responsible roles for each step are identified
- [ ] Efficiency issues or delays are noted
- [ ] Communication channels between teams are effective

### Governance and Compliance

- [ ] Content is reviewed for brand tone and accuracy
- [ ] Legal or regulatory reviews are integrated into the workflow
- [ ] Privacy and data handling policies are reflected in content
- [ ] Escalation processes for sensitive topics exist

### Documentation and Tools

- [ ] Standard operating procedures (SOPs) are available
- [ ] Content guidelines and style guides are accessible
- [ ] Tools used for content creation and workflow management are documented
- [ ] There is a training plan for new content contributors
