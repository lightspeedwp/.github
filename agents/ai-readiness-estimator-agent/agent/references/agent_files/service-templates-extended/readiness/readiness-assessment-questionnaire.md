## Section 1: Content

1. What are your primary content assets (e.g. product pages, FAQs, documentation)?
2. Who owns and maintains these assets?
3. How often is content reviewed or updated?
4. Are there any known content gaps or inaccuracies?
5. Do you maintain structured metadata or schema on your pages?

## Section 2: Workflows

1. Describe the workflows for publishing and updating content.
2. How are approvals managed (e.g. editorial, legal, brand)?
3. What tools are used to manage workflows (e.g. WordPress, Asana, custom systems)?
4. Are there any bottlenecks or pain points in current workflows?

## Section 3: Technical

1. What platforms and plugins power your website?
2. Are there any custom integrations or APIs in use?
3. How is staging and deployment handled?
4. Do you use caching, CDNs, or performance optimisation tools?
5. Are there any known technical limitations that could affect AI implementation?

## Section 4: Governance and Policies

1. Do you have existing guidelines for content quality, brand tone, or legal compliance?
2. Are there policies for handling personal data, privacy, and security?
3. Who approves the use of new technologies or vendors?
4. Have any AI or automation tools been evaluated or implemented before?

## Section 5: Objectives and Risks

1. What benefits do you hope to realise from AI adoption?
2. What concerns or risks are front of mind?
3. Are there any regulatory or industry‑specific considerations to be aware of?

## Additional Notes
