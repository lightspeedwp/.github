1. **Website and content:** Describe the current website structure and key content assets.

3. **Governance and policies:** Note any existing guidelines governing content, privacy, or AI use.

1. **Approved sources:** What specific pages, documents, or data will the AI be allowed to use?  
2. **Restricted topics:** Are there any topics that the AI must avoid?  
3. **Escalation owners:** Who will handle questions the AI cannot answer or should not answer?  
4. **Privacy/logging concerns:** Are there any privacy or logging policies that must be adhered to?
