# Pre‑Workshop Stakeholder Questionnaire

Use this questionnaire to gather input from workshop participants ahead of a Responsible AI governance workshop.  
Responses will help tailor the agenda and focus areas.

- **Name:** {{stakeholder.name}}
- **Role/department:** {{stakeholder.role}}

## Current AI Usage

1. Describe any AI tools or services your team currently uses.
2. What benefits or problems have you observed from these AI tools?
3. What concerns do you have about how AI is currently used in the organisation?

## Expectations for the Workshop

1. What questions do you hope the workshop will answer?
2. Are there specific risks or opportunities you want to discuss?
3. What outcomes would make this workshop valuable for you?

## Governance and Policies

1. Are you aware of any existing policies or guidelines governing AI use?  
2. What areas of governance (e.g. privacy, security, brand, compliance) are most relevant to your work?  
3. Who should be responsible for reviewing and approving AI-driven activities in your area?

## Additional Comments

Please provide any other information, concerns, or topics you would like the workshop to address.
