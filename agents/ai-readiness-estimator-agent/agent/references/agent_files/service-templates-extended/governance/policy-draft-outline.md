# Responsible AI Policy Draft Outline

This outline provides a starting structure for drafting a Responsible AI policy.  
Adapt sections as needed to reflect the organisation’s values, regulatory environment, and operational requirements.

## 1. Purpose

Explain why the organisation is adopting a Responsible AI policy and what outcomes it seeks to achieve.

## 2. Scope

Define which systems, processes, and teams are covered by this policy (e.g. websites, marketing, support, internal tools).  
Identify any exclusions or related policies that intersect with AI governance.

## 3. Principles

List the core principles guiding AI use, such as:

- Human oversight and accountability
- Transparency and disclosure
- Fairness and non‑discrimination
- Privacy and data protection
- Safety and reliability

## 4. Approved Uses

Describe the acceptable use cases for AI within the organisation and the types of activities that are encouraged.

## 5. Prohibited Uses

List the uses of AI that are expressly prohibited (e.g. decisions that determine eligibility for services, AI impersonation of humans, unsupervised medical advice).

## 6. Governance Structure

Detail who is responsible for AI oversight, decision‑making, and approvals.  
Describe how stakeholders should raise questions, concerns, or requests.

## 7. Review and Escalation

Specify when AI outputs or actions require human review and outline escalation paths for sensitive scenarios.  
Reference the review and escalation matrix.

## 8. Training and Awareness

Describe any training requirements for staff using AI tools and how awareness of the policy will be maintained.

## 9. Compliance and Monitoring

Detail how adherence to the policy will be monitored, including audits, regular reviews, and reporting.

## 10. Updates and Maintenance

Explain how the policy will be updated, who approves changes, and how changes will be communicated.
