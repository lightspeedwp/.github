## Known Context

- Client is starting a new website delivery project.
- Client is a South African financial planning firm.
- Current website is outdated.
- Planned target platform is WordPress.
- Core rebuild goals are lead generation, trust, and search visibility.
- A public-facing chatbot may be considered later in the roadmap.
- Governance and source-of-truth controls matter because the client operates in a regulated sector.
- Available materials at this stage:
  - vague brief only
- Missing or unconfirmed foundation items:
  - website URL
  - current sitemap or key service pages
  - content owner
  - final approver
  - legal or privacy reviewer
  - approved source-of-truth materials
  - policy pages and disclosure wording
  - FAQ source suitable for chatbot grounding
  - analytics/Search Console access or baseline performance data
  - approval workflow for AI-assisted drafting or chatbot content

## Stage Call

- Primary stage: Kickoff and scoping
- Delivery posture: Strict-mode discovery because this is a financial services website with possible future chatbot scope
- Earliest unresolved stage that materially matters next: source inventory with lightweight readiness and governance discovery

## Material Risks

- Financial planning is a regulated or sensitive context, so governance, privacy and approval controls need attention early.
- No confirmed source-of-truth owner means draft content could be reused as if approved.
- No confirmed policy or disclosure content weakens trust and may create compliance review risk later.
- Chatbot planning should not move beyond high-level scoping until approved FAQs, policies and escalation routes exist.
- An outdated WordPress site can also indicate plugin, security, maintenance and structured-content debt that may affect migration scope.

## Recommended Next Deliverable

- Create a project intake and source-inventory starter pack from the current brief.
- Ask only the minimum next-round questions needed to confirm owners, sources, approvals, baseline performance and launch intent.
- Follow that with a lightweight readiness and governance discovery pass before any chatbot recommendation.
