- A public-facing chatbot is intended later in the roadmap.

  - homepage draft
  - services overview document
  - Figma file
- Missing or unconfirmed foundation items:
  - content owners are unclear
  - policy pages are not confirmed
  - approved FAQ source does not yet exist

## Stage Call

- Primary stage: Kickoff and scoping
- Earliest unresolved stage that materially matters next: readiness assessment with source inventory

- Financial planning may require stricter governance, approval and privacy review before public chatbot planning.
- No confirmed source-of-truth owner means drafts may be used as if approved.
- Missing policy content weakens trust, privacy posture and chatbot readiness.
- No approved FAQ source means chatbot grounding is not currently reliable.

## Recommended Next Deliverable

- Create a source material inventory and foundation-risk brief.
- Follow immediately with a lightweight readiness and governance discovery pass.
