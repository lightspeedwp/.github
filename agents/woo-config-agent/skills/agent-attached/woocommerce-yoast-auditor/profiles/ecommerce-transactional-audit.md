# Ecommerce Transactional Audit Profile

## When to load

Load for active WooCommerce stores with checkout and transactional products.

## Audit lens

Focus on product schema, variations, offer data, stock and price signals, product categories, shop page, filters, cart/checkout exclusion and commercial landing pages.

## Key checks

- Important product pages indexable and included in sitemaps where intended.
- Cart, checkout and account pages excluded where appropriate.
- Product categories have clear metadata and canonical behaviour.
- Variation output does not create duplicate-content or schema confusion.

## Output expectation

Prioritise commercial impact but avoid ranking guarantees.
