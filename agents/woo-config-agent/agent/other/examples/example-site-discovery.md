# Example Site Discovery

## Business basics

- Brand name: Example Store Co.
- Primary offer: WooCommerce-based product sales
- Ideal customer: Online shoppers comparing products before purchase
- Main conversion goal: Completed purchases and qualified pre-sales enquiries

## Site structure

- Homepage focus: Trust, featured products, and category navigation
- Blog or news naming: Journal
- Key navigation sections: Shop, Categories, About, FAQs, Contact, Cart

## Content model

- Post types: Products, Pages, Posts, FAQs
- Taxonomies: Product categories, product tags, product attributes

## Forms and SEO

- Required forms: Contact, product enquiry, wholesale enquiry
- Priority landing pages: Shop, featured category pages, product pages, shipping and returns, contact
