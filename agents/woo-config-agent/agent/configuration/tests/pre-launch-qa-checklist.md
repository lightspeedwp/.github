## Store identity and settings

- [ ] Store name matches the approved brand
- [ ] Store email sender details are correct
- [ ] Currency, selling regions, and customer locations are configured correctly
- [ ] Tax posture is confirmed

## Core store pages

- [ ] Shop page is assigned correctly
- [ ] Cart page works correctly
- [ ] Checkout page works correctly
- [ ] My Account page works correctly
- [ ] Privacy Policy, Terms, and Refunds or returns pages are present and linked where needed

## Catalogue

- [ ] Product categories are structured clearly
- [ ] Attributes and variations are configured consistently
- [ ] Product titles, descriptions, prices, and images are launch-ready
- [ ] Stock status and availability messaging are correct

## Payments, shipping, and tax

- [ ] Intended payment methods are enabled and tested
- [ ] Shipping zones and methods are configured correctly
- [ ] Shipping rates, thresholds, and exceptions are checked
- [ ] Tax classes and display behaviour are checked
- [ ] Coupons or promotional logic are tested if in scope

## Checkout and customer communications

- [ ] Guest checkout and account-creation behaviour are correct
- [ ] Checkout fields and messaging are appropriate
- [ ] Order confirmation emails are sent successfully
- [ ] Sender name, sender address, and notification routing are correct

## Launch checks

- [ ] Product, cart, and checkout flows are tested in incognito mode
- [ ] Product, cart, and checkout flows are tested on mobile
- [ ] Staging review is complete before launch
- [ ] Known risks, blockers, and manual follow-ups are documented
