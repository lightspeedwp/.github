## Active

- WooCommerce launch readiness: validate the now-active WooCommerce stack before any production launch decision.
- Confirm front-end behaviour for Shop, Cart, Checkout and My Account pages now that assigned page IDs resolve.
- Validate payment gateways, shipping zones, tax posture/rates and transactional email routing end to end.
- Review published product catalogue for prices, SKUs, stock, category hygiene, duplicate draft/import records, image alt text and SEO metadata.
- Triage Accessibility Checker issues on WooCommerce-relevant pages before launch.

## Blocked

- Production launch sign-off is blocked until checkout, payment, shipping, tax and email behaviour are tested from the front end.

## Follow-ups

- Run front-end checkout QA in incognito and on mobile for South Africa/ZAR purchasing paths.
- Confirm whether the enabled international shipping zone is deliberate before launch.
- Confirm whether the active Disable Emails plugin is intentionally allowing mail or should be deactivated for transactional email testing.
