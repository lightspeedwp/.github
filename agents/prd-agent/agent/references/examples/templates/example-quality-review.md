# Quality review

## Overall assessment

The planning pack is directionally strong and well structured, but it is not yet fully ready for downstream estimation or delivery commitment because several evidence gaps remain unresolved.

## What is strong

- The problem statement is clear and grounded in observed editorial pain points.
- Scope and non-scope are separated cleanly.
- Key workflow and discoverability goals are visible.
- Risks and dependencies are already surfaced rather than hidden.
- The technical brief identifies where implementation certainty is still limited.

## What is weak or missing

- Accessibility baseline evidence is still missing.
- Analytics baseline for content discovery is not yet documented.
- Editorial workflow priorities have not been formally confirmed by stakeholders.
- Source-of-truth design direction is still only partially validated.
- The current repository constraints have not been reviewed deeply enough for confident estimation.

## Contradictions and risks

- The planning pack assumes the current theme can be extended, but that has not yet been validated through deeper repository review.
- The stated delivery confidence is stronger than the available baseline evidence supports.
- If stakeholder workflow priorities change after technical planning begins, scope drift is likely.

## Readiness judgment

Ready for: stronger planning refinement and deeper technical validation.

Not yet ready for: reliable final estimate or full delivery commitment.

## Recommended fixes

- Gather accessibility and analytics baseline evidence.
- Confirm stakeholder workflow priorities explicitly.
- Validate the design source of truth.
- Review the repository constraints in more detail before estimate sign-off.
- Re-run estimate readiness after those evidence gaps are closed.
