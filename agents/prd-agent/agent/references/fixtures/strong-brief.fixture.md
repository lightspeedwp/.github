# Strong brief fixture

## Scenario

A planning brief exists and is strong enough to support downstream PRD refinement or technical planning.

## Strengths

- business goal is clear and specific
- stakeholder and user context are documented
- scope and non-scope are separated cleanly
- constraints and dependencies are visible
- assumptions are explicit rather than hidden
- major risks and open questions are surfaced

## Expected use

Use this fixture to test progression from planning brief to PRD drafting, technical-brief preparation, and stronger readiness assessment.
