## Surface

Publishing website homepage

## Goal

Improve headline hierarchy, story discoverability, reading comfort, and subscription conversion without hurting editorial flexibility.

## Strengths

- The homepage supports multiple editorial content tiers, including lead stories, secondary stories, and promotional modules.
- The page already includes subscription prompts and navigation cues that can be improved without changing the core content model.

## Risks

### Risk 1

- Risk: The visual hierarchy between lead story, secondary stories, and promotional content is weak.
- Severity: high
- Evidence: Priority tiers do not feel distinct enough, which makes the lead story less unmistakable than it should be.
- Area: hierarchy

### Risk 2

- Risk: Dense homepage blocks make scanning harder, especially on mobile and for older readers.
- Severity: high
- Evidence: Content groupings and spacing make headline and metadata scanning feel visually heavy.
- Area: readability

### Risk 3

- Risk: Subscription prompts interrupt content discovery instead of supporting it.
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: Conversion modules compete with reading intent rather than following it.
- Area: conversion

### Risk 4

- Risk: Navigation and section labels do not make priority content paths obvious.
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: Discovery cues do not clearly signal where readers should go next.
- Area: navigation

## Accessibility gaps

- Check text contrast on secondary headlines and metadata.
- Verify heading structure and reading order for screen readers.
- Review tap target size and spacing on mobile navigation and promotional elements.

## Recommendations

- Strengthen the contrast between editorial priority tiers so the lead story is unmistakable.
- Improve spacing, grouping, and headline rhythm to reduce visual density.
- Reposition or reframe subscription prompts so they support rather than interrupt reading intent.
- Clarify section labels and navigation cues to improve discovery.

## Open questions

- Which business goal matters most on the homepage: subscriptions, article discovery, or sponsor visibility?
- Which promo modules are contractually fixed versus negotiable?
