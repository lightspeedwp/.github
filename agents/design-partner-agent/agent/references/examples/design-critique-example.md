## Artifact

Mobile WooCommerce product page

## Goal

Increase product clarity, trust, and add-to-cart confidence on mobile.

## Top issues

### Issue 1

- Issue: The primary add-to-cart action competes with secondary actions and metadata.
- Severity: high
- Evidence: CTA prominence is diluted by nearby secondary actions and surrounding information density.
- Impact: Users may hesitate or miss the main purchase path at the key decision point.

### Issue 2

- Issue: Shipping and returns details are not visible early enough to support purchase confidence.
- Severity: high
- Evidence: Trust-building purchase details appear too late in the scroll path relative to price and CTA content.
- Impact: Unanswered trust questions may reduce add-to-cart confidence.

### Issue 3

- Issue: Product information blocks feel dense on mobile and reduce scanability.
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: Content spacing and hierarchy make it harder to distinguish key product details quickly.
- Impact: Slower scanning can weaken comprehension and confidence.

## Recommendations

### Recommendation 1

- Recommendation: Strengthen the visual hierarchy of the primary CTA.
- Priority: high
- Tradeoffs: Secondary actions may need to move lower in the layout or lose emphasis.

### Recommendation 2

- Recommendation: Surface shipping and returns information closer to price and CTA content.
- Priority: high
- Tradeoffs: Above-the-fold density may increase if trust details are not integrated carefully.

### Recommendation 3

- Recommendation: Reduce content density and improve spacing rhythm for mobile reading.
- Priority: medium
- Tradeoffs: Some lower-priority merchandising or supporting details may need to move further down the page.

## Accessibility notes

- Check contrast on secondary text.
- Verify focus visibility for quantity and add-to-cart controls.

## Open questions

- Are returns and delivery timelines the top trust concerns for this client?
- Which supporting details need to remain visible above the fold?
