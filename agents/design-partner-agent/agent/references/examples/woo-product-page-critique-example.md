## Artifact

Mobile WooCommerce product page

## Goal

Improve trust, scanability, and add-to-cart confidence on mobile.

## Top issues

### Issue 1

- Issue: The primary add-to-cart action is visually weaker than surrounding content.
- Severity: high
- Evidence: Competing nearby elements reduce the prominence of the main purchase action at the key decision moment.
- Impact: Users may hesitate or miss the clearest path to purchase.

### Issue 2

- Issue: Shipping and returns information is buried.
- Severity: high
- Evidence: Important trust-building details appear too late to support decision-making before commitment.
- Impact: Missing reassurance can reduce confidence and increase drop-off.

### Issue 3

- Issue: Mobile spacing makes the page harder to scan.
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: Dense vertical rhythm and limited separation between information blocks reduce readability on smaller screens.
- Impact: Slower scanning makes it harder for users to compare details and act confidently.

## Recommendations

### Recommendation 1

- Recommendation: Strengthen CTA prominence and reduce competing visual weight nearby.
- Priority: high
- Tradeoffs: Secondary actions or supporting content may need to move lower or lose emphasis.

### Recommendation 2

- Recommendation: Surface shipping, returns, and trust signals earlier.
- Priority: high
- Tradeoffs: Above-the-fold density may increase unless trust content is integrated carefully.

### Recommendation 3

- Recommendation: Simplify vertical rhythm and reduce clutter on mobile.
- Priority: medium
- Tradeoffs: Some supporting merchandising or informational content may need to move further down the page.

## Accessibility notes

- Check CTA contrast and focus visibility.
- Verify heading structure and screen-reader labelling.

## Open questions

- Which trust details matter most for this client: shipping speed, returns, payment safety, or something else?
- Which supporting content must remain visible near the CTA on mobile?
