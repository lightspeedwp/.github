## Objective

Improve the WooCommerce category page so product discovery feels clearer, faster, and more trustworthy on mobile and desktop without requiring a broader storefront redesign.

## Goals

- Improve product discovery and category navigation.
- Reduce friction in filtering and sorting.
- Make high-intent browsing clearer on mobile.

## Audience

- WooCommerce website owners responsible for merchandising and conversion.
- Online shoppers browsing category pages on mobile and desktop.

## Messaging priorities

- Make the category page feel easy to scan and compare.
- Reinforce that filters and sorting help users narrow choices quickly.
- Keep promotional content supportive rather than dominant.

## Recommended structure

- Clear category heading with concise supporting context.
- Early filtering and sorting controls with strong scanability.
- Product grid with consistent merchandising hierarchy.
- Optional promotional or educational content placed after the primary browsing task.
- Clear continuation paths into product detail pages.

## Requirements and constraints

- Must work within an existing WordPress and WooCommerce setup.
- Filtering behavior may be limited by current plugin architecture.
- Content and merchandising blocks still need to support promotions.
- The work is scoped to the category page, not a full storefront rebrand.

## Open questions

- Which category filters matter most to conversion?
- How much merchandising content must remain above the fold?
- Are there technical limits on sticky filters or inline filter summaries?

## Recommended next artifact

Create a bounded category-page critique or redesign direction that tests filtering clarity, scanability, and merchandising hierarchy before moving into implementation handoff.
