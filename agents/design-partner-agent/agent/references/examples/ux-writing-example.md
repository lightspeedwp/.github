## Surface or moment

Tour booking inquiry form submission confirmation

## User goal

Understand whether the inquiry was sent successfully and what happens next.

## Current copy

"Your form has been submitted."

## Problems with current copy

- It confirms the action but does not explain the next step.
- It does not reassure the user about timing or expected follow-up.
- It feels generic and does not build trust in a high-intent travel context.

## Recommended copy options

### Option 1

- Copy: "Your inquiry has been sent. Our team will review your details and get back to you shortly with the next steps."
- Why it works: It confirms success and sets a clear expectation without sounding overly formal.

### Option 2

- Copy: "Thanks, your inquiry is on its way. We’ll review it and follow up soon with availability and next steps."
- Why it works: It sounds warm and reassuring while giving the user more clarity about what happens next.

## Tone and brand notes

- Keep the wording clear, supportive, and low-jargon.
- Avoid overpromising exact timing unless the business can support it.
- Match LightSpeed's plain-spoken, helpful tone.

## Accessibility and clarity checks

- Keep the message easy to understand at a glance.
- Avoid vague success messaging with no next-step guidance.
- Ensure the confirmation remains meaningful if read out of surrounding UI context.

## Implementation notes

- Pair the message with a visible success state.
- If follow-up timing is known, the copy can be made more specific.
