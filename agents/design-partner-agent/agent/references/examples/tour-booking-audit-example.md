# Tour Booking Audit Example

## Surface

Tour booking flow

## Goal

Improve booking flow UX clarity and mobile scanability.

## Strengths

- The flow already surfaces key decision-making content such as pricing, itinerary details, and booking calls to action.
- The journey is bounded enough to support targeted UX improvements without a full redesign.

## Risks

### Risk 1

- Risk: Pricing context is not clear enough before commitment points.
- Severity: high
- Evidence: Users may reach commitment moments before understanding inclusions, exclusions, or pricing structure.
- Area: pricing clarity

### Risk 2

- Risk: Itinerary content is dense and hard to scan.
- Severity: medium
- Evidence: Long blocks of itinerary information make the flow harder to review quickly on mobile.
- Area: content hierarchy

### Risk 3

- Risk: Booking CTAs compete with secondary content.
- Severity: high
- Evidence: Primary booking actions do not stand out clearly enough from surrounding supporting content.
- Area: conversion

## Accessibility gaps

- Review mobile readability for long itinerary sections.
- Check touch target size and spacing around primary booking actions.
- Ensure pricing and CTA content remain understandable when scanned quickly on smaller screens.

## Recommendations

- Clarify inclusions, exclusions, and pricing structure earlier.
- Improve itinerary hierarchy with clearer chunking and labels.
- Increase CTA visibility and consistency across mobile steps.

## Open questions

- Are pricing rules fixed or flexible by season?
- What are the most common drop-off points in the booking flow?
