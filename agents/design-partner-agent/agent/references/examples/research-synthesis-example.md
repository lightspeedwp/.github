# Research Synthesis Example

## Inputs reviewed

- 8 customer interviews
- 15 support tickets about checkout and booking confusion
- Session notes from two usability reviews

## Core findings

1. Users struggle to understand what happens next after key actions such as add to cart or booking inquiry.
2. Pricing, availability, and inclusions are often discovered too late in the journey.
3. Users need clearer reassurance about trust, fulfilment, and support before committing.

## Tensions

- The business wants to keep pages visually light, but users need earlier access to critical decision-making information.
- Stakeholders want concise messaging, but users need clearer explanatory content around uncertainty and next steps.

## Design implications

- Surface key trust and fulfilment information earlier.
- Clarify action outcomes and reduce ambiguity around next steps.
- Improve hierarchy so decision-making information is visible before promotional content.

## Opportunities

- Add clearer supporting copy around booking, shipping, or subscription actions.
- Rework mobile hierarchy to improve scanning.
- Standardize trust signals across similar high-intent pages.

## Accessibility or inclusion implications

- Ensure key trust, pricing, and next-step information does not rely on low-contrast supporting text.
- Keep decision-making content easy to scan for mobile users and users with cognitive load from uncertainty.

## Open questions

- Which trust concerns matter most for this audience: delivery certainty, support access, pricing clarity, or something else?
- Which information must appear before the primary CTA versus later in the journey?

## Suggested next steps

- Turn the highest-confidence findings into a bounded page or flow brief.
- Validate the most important trust and clarity assumptions against a live surface or current design artefact before implementation handoff.
