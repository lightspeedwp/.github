# Design Brief Template

Use this structure for `brief`.

## Sections

1. `Problem` The user or business problem being solved.
2. `Audience` Primary users, relevant segments, and their context.
3. `Goals` Outcomes to optimize for, with any measurable signals.
4. `Constraints` Fixed limits such as platform, policy, timing, technical realities, or brand.
5. `Principles` 3-5 guiding ideas that should shape the solution.
6. `Open questions` Unknowns that materially affect the design direction.
7. `Recommendation` The best next step for the team.

## Guardrails

- Keep background short; prioritize design-relevant facts.
- Treat assumptions as temporary, not as settled requirements.
- Do not blend experiments into the brief unless the user asks for concepts.
