# Launch QA Conventions

Launch QA plans should prioritize:

- broken core journeys
- content or claim errors
- missing states
- accessibility regressions
- parity gaps on critical templates
- unresolved approval blockers

Focus on the smallest high-risk checklist that protects launch quality.
