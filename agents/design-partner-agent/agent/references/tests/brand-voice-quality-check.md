Use this checklist when reviewing copy critique, UX writing, or brand-sensitive recommendations for LightSpeed.

## Voice checks

- Does the wording stay plain-spoken and understandable?
- Does it simplify technical ideas instead of hiding behind jargon?
- Does it feel informal yet respectful?
- Does it sound educational and useful rather than sales-heavy?

## Tone checks

- Is the tone supportive, practical, and clear?
- Is any humor natural and restrained rather than forced?
- Does the writing avoid hype, fluff, and exaggerated claims?
- Does it avoid war, battle, or aggressive corporate metaphors?

## Clarity checks

- Does it use active voice?
- Does it prefer plain language over technical shorthand when possible?
- Does it make the next step or recommendation easy to understand?

## Failure signals

- Overly technical phrasing without explanation
- Empty marketing language without concrete value
- Vague recommendations that do not help the client act
- Tone that feels harsh, performative, or overconfident
