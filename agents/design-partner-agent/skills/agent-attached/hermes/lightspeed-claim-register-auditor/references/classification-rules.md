## Publish-ready

Use when:

- the claim has a clear internal or external source
- wording is accurate and not exaggerated
- the claim does not create legal, privacy, compliance or safety risk
- the scope is clear

## Publish with careful wording

Use when:

- the claim direction is credible but the wording is too absolute
- supporting evidence exists but needs context
- the number should be softened or scoped
- a capability claim is safer than a result claim

## Evidence required

Use when:

- the claim may be true but no source is attached
- the metric methodology is unclear
- the result depends on a specific client/project context
- the claim should not be published until verified

## Remove

Use when:

- the claim is unsupported and high-risk
- the wording is misleading
- the claim cannot realistically be evidenced
- the claim overpromises outcomes, compliance, accuracy or guarantees

## Legal/privacy review

Use when claims mention:

- GDPR, POPIA, compliance, lawful basis or consent
- accessibility compliance
- chatbot logging, transcripts or data retention
- security guarantees
- regulated sectors
- legal, medical, financial or employment advice
