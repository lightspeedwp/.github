## Core rule

Use approved claims only. Do not convert draft stats or internal notes into public facts unless the source marks them as approved.

## Claim confidence tiers

| Tier | How to use |
|---|---|
| Publish-ready | Can be used in public copy with source retained |
| Publish with careful wording | Can be used, but avoid absolute wording and explain context |
| Evidence required | Do not use as a factual claim; flag for review |
| Internal only | Do not publish or use in chatbot output |

## Claims that need special care

Treat these as evidence-required unless directly verified:

- AI citation increases
- chatbot resolution rates
- prediction accuracy
- featured snippet growth
- guaranteed traffic increases
- ROI claims
- revenue uplifts
- exact speed improvements without test evidence
- legal or compliance guarantees

## Rewrite patterns

| Risky wording | Safer wording |
|---|---|
| guaranteed AI citations | improves the signals AI systems can interpret and cite |
| 100% compliant | designed to support compliance workflows, subject to review |
| 89% chatbot resolution | set a measurable pilot target after source approval |
| 92% prediction accuracy | AI-assisted forecasting with documented assumptions |
| 400% more snippets | improve eligibility for rich and answer-led results |
| zero risk | reduce risk through review, testing and governance |
| fully automated | AI-assisted with human review and approval |

## Internal notes requirement

When a claim is used, list:

- claim wording
- source
- confidence tier
- owner
- page location
- review date or required review
