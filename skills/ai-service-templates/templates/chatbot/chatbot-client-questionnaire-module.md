## Client

- Client: {{client.name}}
- Website: {{client.website_url}}

## Chatbot Goal

1. What should the chatbot help users do?
2. What should the chatbot not help users do?
3. Who is the primary audience?
4. What business outcome matters most?

## Source Content

1. Which website pages or documents should the chatbot use?
2. Who owns and approves that content?
3. What content is outdated, missing, or risky?
4. Are there topics the chatbot must avoid?

## Lead Capture and Handoff

1. Should the chatbot collect enquiries?
2. What fields are required?
3. Where should enquiries go?
4. Who responds to escalations?

## Privacy and Logging

1. Will conversation logs be stored?
2. Who can access them?
3. How long should they be retained?
4. What personal information should not be requested?
