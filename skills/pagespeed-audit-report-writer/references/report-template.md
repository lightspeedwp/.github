Use this only when the user asks for a reusable template or when evidence is too incomplete for a measured report.

```markdown
# PageSpeed Audit Report: [Client / Website]

**Audit date:** [Date]  
**Prepared for:** [Client / Team]  
**Prepared by:** [Name / Team]  
**Audited page(s):** [URL list]  
**Audit source:** [PageSpeed Insights / Lighthouse / WebPageTest / Other]

## Executive Summary

[Plain-language summary of the overall performance position, biggest risks, and most useful next actions. Do not add metrics unless supplied.]

## Client Context

[Short context about the site, audience, page goals, and why speed matters. Label assumptions clearly.]

## Audit Scope

| Page | URL | Device | Evidence Supplied | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [Page name] | [URL] | [Mobile/Desktop] | [Metrics/screenshots/summary] | [Notes] |

## Prioritized Findings

### Finding: [Clear finding title]

**Affected page(s):** [Page names or URLs]  
**Priority:** [High / Medium / Low]  
**Effort:** [Quick win / Medium effort / Larger engineering work]  

**Symptom:** [What was observed.]  
**Why it matters:** [User/business/SEO/conversion impact.]  
**Likely cause:** [Use cautious wording unless proven.]  
**Evidence:** [Measured metric, audit note, screenshot reference, or supplied observation.]  
**Recommended fix:** [Practical next action and validation method.]

## Quick Wins

[Supported low-risk recommendations.]

## Medium-Effort Improvements

[Supported recommendations needing planning, QA, or developer work.]

## Larger Engineering Work

[Supported deeper engineering or infrastructure recommendations.]

## Limitations

[Missing data, unknown test settings, lab-vs-field caveats, untested pages, or incomplete evidence.]

## Before / After Comparison

| Page | Metric | Before | After | Change | Notes |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| [Page] | [Metric] | [Value] | [Value] | [Change] | [Notes] |

## Recommended Next Steps

1. [Next action]
2. [Next action]
3. [Next action]
```
