Use this example for a simple paid event registration where Gravity Forms collects attendee details and a payment add-on takes payment. Route to WooCommerce or a ticketing/event platform when inventory, seat selection, order management, tax, fulfilment or refunds are complex.

## Intended flow

1. Visitor chooses the event ticket type and quantity.
2. Total is visible before submission.
3. Attendee contact details are collected.
4. Payment feed runs after branch validation.
5. Confirmation explains whether the registration is confirmed, pending payment, or awaiting manual review.
6. Admin/event owner receives a limited notification.

## Guardrails

- Confirm capacity and refund policy before launch.
- Do not treat entry creation alone as paid registration unless payment status is successful.
- Keep attendee and payment data minimised.
- Test quantity caps, sold-out/closed states if those are managed outside Gravity Forms.
