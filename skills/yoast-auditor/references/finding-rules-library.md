## When to load

Load when classifying findings, scoring an audit, reviewing a draft report, or checking whether claims are overstated.

## What it helps decide

Assign Critical, High, Medium or Low priority using evidence, business impact and confidence.

## Priority rules

### Critical

Use for issues that can seriously affect crawlability, indexation, canonical handling, redirect safety, search visibility of important pages or major WooCommerce product discoverability.

Examples: important pages noindexed, wrong canonicals, broken important sitemaps, robots blocking key content, unresolved launch redirect risk, product pages with serious indexation/schema risk.

### High

Use for likely impact on important pages, product pages, structured data, social sharing, metadata consistency, taxonomy archive performance or WooCommerce discoverability.

### Medium

Use for issues affecting quality, consistency, discoverability, reporting clarity or editorial workflow, but unlikely to block core SEO behaviour.

### Low

Use for housekeeping, documentation gaps, minor metadata quality issues or optional workflow improvements.

## Scoring

Start at 100. Subtract Critical -25, High -10, Medium -5, Low -2. Minimum score is 0. State the reviewed scope beside the score.

## Evidence discipline

Label content judgement separately. Do not promise ranking gains. Say "may improve search appearance clarity" rather than "will improve rankings".

## Output expectations

Each finding should include title, priority, confidence, evidence, risk, recommendation, owner/next route and limitation.
