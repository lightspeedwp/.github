# Local Business Audit Profile

## When to load

Load when the client has physical locations, local visibility matters, or Local SEO output is explicitly in scope.

## Audit lens

Treat Local SEO gaps as business information gaps unless Local SEO evidence is in scope and the business relies on local visibility.

## Key checks

- Consistency of business name, address, phone, hours and location details where visible.
- LocalBusiness schema evidence if present.
- Contact/location page metadata.
- Map or location page output where relevant.

## Safety note

Do not propose changing legal business details, addresses, opening hours or phone numbers without clear source evidence and approval.
