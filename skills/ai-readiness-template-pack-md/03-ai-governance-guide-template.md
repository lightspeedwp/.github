Target audience: Client leadership, marketing teams, content editors, support teams, product owners, WordPress administrators and implementation teams.  
Purpose: Define how AI may be used for website content, chatbot behaviour and supporting workflows in a practical, safe and maintainable way.  
Recommended format: Client-facing governance guide, internal operating document, onboarding document or project handover document.  
Suggested CTA: Approve this guide before AI-assisted content publishing or chatbot launch.

> This guide is an operational template, not legal advice. Confirm legal, privacy, employment, consumer protection, sector-specific and data protection obligations with a qualified adviser.

### Writing rules

- Use plain UK English unless the client has a specific regional requirement.
- Prioritise clarity over cleverness.
- Avoid unsupported claims, exaggerated results and vague AI hype.
- Explain complex topics in steps.
- Use active voice where possible.
- Avoid jargon unless the audience expects it.
- Keep paragraphs short for readability.
- Use headings, lists and tables to improve scanning.
- Include evidence, examples or links where claims need support.
- Do not invent statistics, testimonials, awards, accreditations, prices or policies.

### Accessibility and inclusive language rules

- Use descriptive link text.
- Use meaningful headings.
- Avoid relying on colour alone.
- Provide useful alt text for informative images.
- Avoid unnecessary emojis in critical instructions.
- Do not use language that excludes or stereotypes groups.
- Keep chatbot responses concise and readable.
- Provide alternatives when users cannot complete an action in chat.

### Publishing checklist

Before publishing AI-assisted content, confirm:

- [ ] The content has a clear page purpose and audience.
- [ ] The content matches the approved voice and tone.
- [ ] Claims are supported by evidence.
- [ ] Prices, dates, services, product details and policies are accurate.
- [ ] The content does not include confidential or unauthorised data.
- [ ] Legal, privacy or regulated claims have been reviewed where needed.
- [ ] SEO basics are complete: title, meta description, headings, internal links and search intent.
- [ ] Structured data requirements are confirmed where relevant.
- [ ] Images include appropriate file names, captions and alt text.
- [ ] Accessibility checks are complete.
- [ ] Human approval has been recorded.
- [ ] Review date and owner are set.

### Required fallback response

Use when the chatbot is unsure, when the answer is not in approved sources or when the topic is outside scope.

> I do not have enough reliable information to answer that confidently. I can help you contact the team so they can confirm the right answer.

### Required escalation response

Use when a human needs to take over.

> This needs a team member to review. Please share your contact details, or use the contact route below, and the right person will follow up.

### Required "I do not know" response

Use when the chatbot has no approved source.

> I do not know based on the information I am allowed to use. I do not want to guess. Please contact the team for confirmation.

### Prohibited chatbot behaviour

- Do not invent answers, policies, prices, availability, guarantees or technical claims.
- Do not provide professional advice where the business is not authorised to do so.
- Do not ask for sensitive personal data unless the workflow has been approved.
- Do not continue a high-risk conversation when escalation is required.
- Do not hide that the user is interacting with AI.
- Do not rely on unapproved or outdated source material.
- Do not promise outcomes the business cannot guarantee.

## G. Example prompts and responses

### Approved AI writing prompt template

```text
You are writing for [client name]. Use UK English. Write in a [tone] tone for [audience].

Goal: [page/post/email purpose]
Source material: [paste or link approved source material]
Must include: [key messages, proof points, CTA]
Must avoid: [banned claims, words, topics]
Format: [headings, bullets, table, FAQ, etc.]
Review requirements: Do not invent facts, prices, testimonials, policies or statistics. Flag anything that needs human confirmation.
```

### Approved content summary prompt

```text
Summarise the following approved source material for a website editor. Keep the summary factual, concise and suitable for internal planning. Separate confirmed facts from items that need human confirmation.
```

### Approved SEO brief prompt

```text
Create an SEO content brief for [page topic] using only the approved source material below. Include audience, search intent, recommended headings, internal links, FAQ opportunities, schema suggestions and content risks. Do not invent claims or keywords that are not supported by the brief.
```

### Approved chatbot system instruction template

```text
You are [bot name], the AI assistant for [client name]. Your role is to help users with [approved topics]. Use a [tone] tone and UK English.

Only answer from approved source material. If the answer is not available or you are unsure, say you do not know and offer escalation. Do not invent prices, policies, guarantees, availability, legal advice, medical advice, financial advice or technical commitments.

Disclose that you are an AI assistant. Do not collect sensitive personal information. Escalate when the user asks about [escalation triggers].
```

### Example lead qualification flow

1. Ask what the user needs help with.
2. Confirm the relevant product, service or problem.
3. Ask for location or context only if needed.
4. Ask for contact details only after explaining why.
5. Send the lead to the approved CRM or email route.
6. Confirm expected response time without overpromising.

## H. Ongoing improvement

### Monthly review checklist

- [ ] Review published AI-assisted content for accuracy and performance.
- [ ] Review chatbot logs for unanswered questions, risky outputs and new FAQ opportunities.
- [ ] Check whether source documents are current.
- [ ] Review low-confidence bot answers.
- [ ] Update banned topics, fallback responses and escalation rules if needed.
- [ ] Check analytics and conversion impact.
- [ ] Confirm privacy, retention and consent settings still match current practice.
- [ ] Log changes in the governance document.

### Governance update process

1. Identify the issue or improvement.
2. Confirm the source of truth.
3. Update the relevant content, prompt, rule or source file.
4. Test the change.
5. Record the change owner, date and reason.
6. Approve before public release where required.
