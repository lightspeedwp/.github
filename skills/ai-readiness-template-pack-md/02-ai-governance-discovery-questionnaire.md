Target audience: Clients preparing for an AI readiness assessment, website strategy project, content governance project or chatbot implementation.  
Purpose: Gather the business, content, brand, technical, legal, privacy and AI governance context needed to build a practical action plan.  
Recommended format: Web form, Google Doc, Typeform, Gravity Forms, CRM form or structured workshop worksheet.  
Suggested CTA: Submit the completed questionnaire before your AI Website Readiness Assessment.

> This questionnaire helps LightSpeed understand your context. It is not legal advice. Where legal, privacy or regulatory obligations apply, confirm requirements with a qualified legal or privacy adviser.

## Completion guidance for clients

- Answer what you can. It is fine to write "unknown" where needed.
- Add links to existing documents, pages, folders or dashboards where possible.
- Use uploads for brand guides, policies, sales decks, content inventories, FAQs or chatbot source documents.
- The goal is not to prove that everything is complete. The goal is to identify what should be fixed first.

## Question type key

| Type | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Short answer | One sentence or short paragraph. |
| Long answer | Detailed explanation. |
| Checkbox | Select all that apply. |
| Multiple choice | Select one option. |
| Rating | Score from 1 to 5 unless otherwise stated. |
| Upload | Attach a file or folder link. |
| URL | Link to a website, document, dashboard or system. |
