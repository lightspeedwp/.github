## Landing page outline

### Hero

- Headline: Is your website ready for responsible AI?
- Subheading: Use this practical checklist to assess your content, data, design system, search visibility, governance and chatbot readiness.
- Primary CTA: Download the AI Readiness Checklist
- Secondary CTA: Book an AI Website Readiness Assessment

### Problem section

- AI tools can only perform well when the website, content and governance foundations are reliable.
- Outdated content, unclear ownership, weak privacy controls and poor structure can lead to inaccurate outputs, poor user experience and avoidable risk.
- A website that is fast, accessible, structured and well governed becomes a stronger foundation for AI-assisted content, search visibility and chatbot support.

### What the checklist covers

- Website foundations
- Content readiness
- Search, structured data and AI discoverability
- Brand, UX and design system readiness
- Data, privacy and governance
- Chatbot readiness

### Who it is for

- Business owners
- Marketing managers
- Ecommerce teams
- Professional services firms
- Content-heavy organisations
- Teams considering AI-assisted content or website chatbots

### What happens after download

1. Complete the checklist.
2. Identify your strongest and weakest areas.
3. Book an assessment if you want a practical action plan.
4. Use the deeper questionnaire to support governance, redesign, content, SEO or chatbot planning.

### Trust section

- WordPress strategy
- Content governance
- Accessibility
- Technical SEO
- Performance
- AI governance
- Sustainable implementation

### CTA block

Book an AI Website Readiness Assessment. The checklist gives you a starting score; the assessment turns it into a prioritised roadmap.

## Email nurture sequence

### Email 1: Delivery email

Subject: Your AI Readiness Checklist is ready

Thanks for downloading the AI Readiness Checklist. Use it to score your website across content, search, UX, privacy, governance and chatbot readiness.

Recommended next step: complete the checklist before your next website, content or AI planning meeting. If you want help interpreting the results, book an AI Website Readiness Assessment.

CTA: Book an AI Website Readiness Assessment

### Email 2: Reminder to complete checklist

Subject: Have you scored your website yet?

A useful AI readiness score is not about being perfect. It is about knowing which gaps matter most before you invest in tools, content generation or chatbot implementation.

Pay close attention to low scores in content accuracy, source-of-truth documents, privacy rules, accessibility and chatbot escalation.

CTA: Complete the checklist and book a review

### Email 3: Invitation to book assessment

Subject: Turn your AI readiness score into a practical roadmap

The checklist helps identify symptoms. The assessment helps translate those symptoms into a realistic plan across website foundations, content structure, governance, search, accessibility and AI use cases.

CTA: Book your assessment

### Email 4: Education email about AI governance

Subject: AI governance does not need to be heavy, but it does need to exist

Good AI governance starts with simple decisions: what AI may help with, what it must not do, who reviews outputs, what content it can rely on, and how errors are corrected.

For many businesses, the first step is not a policy document. It is a clearer source of truth for website content, FAQs, services, policies, support processes and brand voice.

CTA: Ask us about an AI governance guide

### Email 5: Case-study style email

Subject: What an AI-ready website project can uncover

A typical readiness review often reveals gaps beyond AI: outdated services, unclear page purpose, missing FAQs, inconsistent CTAs, weak internal linking, accessibility issues, unclear analytics and unsupported chatbot assumptions.

Fixing those gaps improves the website now and gives future AI tools better inputs.

CTA: Book an AI Website Readiness Assessment

### Email 6: Final CTA email

Subject: Ready to make your website AI-ready?

If AI-assisted content, search visibility or chatbot support is on your roadmap, your website needs strong foundations: reliable content, structured pages, clear governance, accessible UX and maintainable WordPress systems.

CTA: Book an AI Website Readiness Assessment

## Recommended tools to mention in the checklist

| Category | Tools / sources | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Performance | Google PageSpeed Insights, Lighthouse, Chrome UX Report, WebPageTest | Check Core Web Vitals, loading, interactivity and visual stability. |
| Accessibility | W3C WAI guidance, WAVE, axe DevTools, Lighthouse accessibility checks, manual keyboard testing | Identify automated and manual accessibility issues. |
| SEO | Google Search Console, Screaming Frog, Sitebulb, Ahrefs, Semrush | Check indexing, crawlability, metadata, internal linking and technical SEO. |
| Structured data | Google Rich Results Test, Schema Markup Validator | Check whether schema is valid and eligible for rich results. |
| Analytics | GA4, Google Tag Manager, Looker Studio, Microsoft Clarity, Hotjar | Understand traffic, conversions, events, user journeys and friction. |
| Privacy/compliance | Privacy policy review, consent management platform, DPIA templates, data mapping spreadsheets | Document data collection, consent, retention and AI-related data handling. |
| Security | WordPress Site Health, Security Headers, SSL Labs, Wordfence, Patchstack, Sucuri | Identify maintenance, update, headers, malware and plugin risk issues. |
| WordPress maintenance | WordPress Site Health, WP-CLI, uptime monitoring, backup logs, plugin/theme inventory | Confirm the site is maintainable and recoverable. |
| AI/chatbot evaluation | Human test scripts, hallucination logs, OWASP LLM checks, prompt injection tests, chatbot analytics | Test bot boundaries, escalation, source accuracy and unsafe responses. |
