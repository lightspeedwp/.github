# Content Collection Checklist Template

Target audience: Clients, project managers, content strategists, designers, WordPress developers and AI/chatbot implementation teams.  
Purpose: Collect all content needed to improve the website, structure key information, support AI-assisted content creation and ground a website chatbot.  
Recommended format: Spreadsheet, Notion table, Google Sheet, Asana project, Airtable base or Markdown checklist.  
Suggested CTA: Complete the priority content items before design, build or chatbot configuration starts.

> This checklist should be adapted after discovery. Do not collect sensitive personal data unless there is a clear, approved reason and the client has confirmed the relevant privacy requirements.

## How to use this checklist

1. Start with the master tracker.
2. Assign an owner to each content item.
3. Mark whether the item exists, needs rewriting, needs review or is missing.
4. Flag whether the item is suitable for chatbot use.
5. Prioritise high-value and high-risk content first.
6. Use project-type add-ons for ecommerce, tourism, professional services, regulated sectors, LMS/membership and multilingual sites.

### AI-source suitability checks

Before content is approved for AI or chatbot use, confirm:

- [ ] It is accurate.
- [ ] It is current.
- [ ] It has a named owner.
- [ ] It has a review date.
- [ ] It does not expose confidential data.
- [ ] It does not include unverified claims.
- [ ] It is clear enough for a non-expert user.
- [ ] It has escalation guidance where needed.
- [ ] It has privacy or legal review where needed.
- [ ] It is stored in a location the project team can maintain.

## Project-type add-ons

### Ecommerce

Add these content items:

- Product names, descriptions and SKUs.
- Product categories and attributes.
- Product images and alt text.
- Pricing, tax and currency rules.
- Stock and availability rules.
- Shipping, delivery and collection information.
- Returns, refunds and warranty policies.
- Payment methods.
- Product recommendation rules.
- Review and testimonial rules.
- Product schema requirements.
- Customer service scripts.

### Professional services

Add these content items:

- Service methodology.
- Qualifications and accreditations.
- Consultant or practitioner bios.
- Case studies and outcomes.
- Compliance claims and disclaimers.
- Pricing principles or engagement models.
- Discovery process.
- Proposal or onboarding process.
- Frequently asked objections.
- Client suitability rules.

### Tourism and hospitality

Add these content items:

- Destinations.
- Itineraries.
- Accommodation details.
- Activities and inclusions.
- Exclusions.
- Seasonal information.
- Availability and booking rules.
- Cancellation policies.
- Travel requirements.
- Accessibility information.
- Location imagery and maps.
- Supplier or partner details.

### Healthcare, finance, legal or other regulated sectors

Add stricter controls:

- Qualified adviser review.
- Disclaimers.
- Evidence register for claims.
- Scope of advice limits.
- Sensitive data handling rules.
- Vulnerable-user handling.
- Escalation and emergency routes.
- Documented approval workflow.
- Higher-frequency content review.
- Chatbot restrictions and fallback rules.

### Membership, LMS or gated content

Add these content items:

- Courses and modules.
- Access rules.
- User roles and permissions.
- Onboarding emails.
- Support paths.
- Learning outcomes.
- Assessment or certification rules.
- Renewal/cancellation rules.
- Member-only knowledge base.
- Community rules.

### Multilingual sites

Add these content items:

- Source language.
- Target languages.
- Localisation rules.
- Translation owner.
- Approved glossary.
- Region-specific legal/privacy wording.
- Local currency, units and date formats.
- Multilingual SEO requirements.
- Translation review workflow.
- Chatbot language fallback rules.

## Suggested next step after completion

Review the completed checklist with the project strategist before design, build or chatbot configuration begins. Any missing high-priority content should be turned into tasks with owners and deadlines.

## Agency-facing notes

- Convert the master tracker into a spreadsheet or Asana template for live projects.
- Use the "Suitable for chatbot?" column to prevent unapproved or outdated content from becoming AI source material.
- Prioritise source-of-truth content before new page design.
- For WordPress projects, map content items to custom post types, taxonomies, block patterns and reusable templates where relevant.
