A generic disclaimer is useful, but chatbot projects still need these operational answers.

## Minimum questions

1. What personal information may the chatbot collect?
2. What personal information must never be collected?
3. Is consent or notice needed before collecting contact details?
4. Where do leads or transcripts go?
5. Who can view conversation logs?
6. How long should logs be retained?
7. Can users request deletion or correction?
8. Are minors, vulnerable users or regulated topics involved?
9. What should the chatbot do when a user shares sensitive information?
10. Who handles privacy or security incidents?

## Standard wording

```markdown
This chatbot planning document supports operational design and risk management. It is not legal advice. Privacy, consent, retention and regulatory requirements should be confirmed with a qualified legal or privacy adviser before launch.
```
