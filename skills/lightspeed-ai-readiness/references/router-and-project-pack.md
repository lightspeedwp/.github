When producing a combined pack, create this folder structure.

```text
[client-slug]-ai-readiness-pack/
├── README.md
├── 01-ai-readiness-assessment.md
├── 02-governance-discovery-summary.md
├── 03-content-collection-checklist.md
├── 04-ai-governance-guide.md
├── 05-chatbot-planning-brief.md
├── 06-roadmap-and-proposal-notes.md
└── source-notes/
    ├── evidence-reviewed.md
    ├── missing-information.md
    └── assumptions-and-risks.md
```

## README template

```markdown
# [Client Name] - AI Readiness Project Pack

## Purpose

This pack summarises the current AI readiness position, governance requirements, content collection needs and chatbot planning recommendations.

## Files

1. AI readiness assessment
2. Governance discovery summary
3. Content collection checklist
4. AI governance guide
5. Chatbot planning brief
6. Roadmap and proposal notes

## Recommended next step

[Insert recommended LightSpeed next step.]

## Disclaimer

This pack supports operational planning and is not legal advice. Legal, privacy and regulatory requirements should be confirmed with a qualified adviser.
```
