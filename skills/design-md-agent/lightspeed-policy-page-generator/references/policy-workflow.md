## 1. Identify the output type

Classify the task as one or more of:

- Privacy Policy brief
- Cookie Policy brief
- Accessibility Statement
- AI Governance page
- chatbot disclosure wording
- data/log retention decision notes
- policy launch checklist

## 2. Identify required evidence

Check whether the user supplied:

- website URL and project stage
- data collection points
- forms and lead capture flows
- analytics and tracking plan
- cookie/consent tooling
- chatbot or AI assistant behaviour
- transcript/log storage details
- accessibility audit notes
- AI governance notes
- review owner

If details are missing, do not invent them. List them as decisions required.

## 3. Draft safely

Write practical policy/trust content in UK English. Prefer clear operational language over legal certainty.

Avoid:

- "fully compliant"
- "guaranteed compliant"
- "no legal risk"
- unsupported data protection claims
- pretending the policy has been legally reviewed

## 4. Add review gates

Every output should end with:

- Review owner
- Missing decisions
- Legal/privacy review required
- Launch status
- Next action
