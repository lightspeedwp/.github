## Scale

Use a 0-10 score per section.

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0-2 | Not in place, unknown or high-risk. |
| 3-4 | Partially present, inconsistent or undocumented. |
| 5-6 | Present but needs improvement before scaling. |
| 7-8 | Good foundation with some governance or quality gaps. |
| 9-10 | Strong, documented, owned and suitable for governed AI adoption. |

## Section criteria

### Website foundations

Assess performance, mobile UX, accessibility, security, maintainability, reusable templates/blocks, forms, analytics and QA.

### Content readiness

Assess accuracy, freshness, completeness, page purpose, source-of-truth documents, FAQs, proof points, ownership and review cadence.

### Search, structured data and AI discoverability

Assess headings, internal linking, metadata, structured data, entity clarity, expertise/trust signals, Search Console monitoring and useful content quality.

### Brand, UX and design system readiness

Assess brand consistency, tone, reusable components, design tokens/theme settings, accessibility, editorial usability and asset organisation.

### Data, privacy and governance

Assess form data, analytics, CRM/ecommerce/email integrations, privacy notices, consent, staff rules for AI tools, human review, error correction and sensitive data handling.

### Chatbot readiness

Assess approved source content, chatbot job definition, boundaries, human escalation, disclosure, data minimisation, transcript review and grounding in site structure.

## Readiness bands

| Percentage | Band | Recommendation |
|---:|---|---|
| 0-30% | Not ready | Prioritise foundations, content ownership, privacy basics and technical remediation. |
| 31-60% | Partly ready | Run a guided assessment and fix high-risk gaps before AI workflows or chatbot pilots. |
| 61-80% | Mostly ready | Create an AI governance plan, tighten ownership and test bounded use cases. |
| 81-100% | Strong foundation | Move into phased governance and implementation planning. |
