Use these line items when turning AI readiness outputs into proposal notes.

## Discovery and strategy

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| AI Website Readiness Assessment | Review website, content, governance and chatbot readiness. | Readiness report and priority roadmap. |
| AI Governance Discovery Workshop | Gather business, content, data, risk and approval context. | Discovery summary and governance inputs. |
| Content and Source Audit | Review existing pages, documents and source quality. | Gap report and source-of-truth register. |

## Foundation work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Website Technical Audit | Identify performance, accessibility, SEO and maintainability issues. | Audit report and remediation backlog. |
| Content Structure and Sitemap Review | Improve page purpose, internal linking and structured content. | Sitemap, page model and content priorities. |
| WordPress Pattern / Template Review | Assess reusable blocks, patterns, theme settings and editor workflow. | Design system and build recommendations. |

## Governance work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| AI Governance Guide | Define approved AI use, exclusions, roles, review and risk controls. | Operational governance guide. |
| AI Content Policy | Lightweight policy for internal content teams. | Policy document. |
| Prompt and Review Workflow Setup | Create approved prompts and review routes. | Prompt templates and workflow rules. |

## Content work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Content Collection Sprint | Gather source material and approvals. | Content tracker and folder pack. |
| Page Brief Creation | Create reusable briefs for priority pages. | Page briefs for homepage, services, products, FAQs and case studies. |
| FAQ and Knowledge Base Development | Build AI-safe source content. | Approved FAQs and source documents. |

## Chatbot work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Chatbot Readiness Review | Decide whether a chatbot should proceed. | Go/no-go brief. |
| Chatbot Behaviour Guide | Define scope, tone, sources, fallback and escalation. | Chatbot operating guide. |
| Chatbot Prototype Planning | Prepare platform, source and testing requirements. | Implementation brief and launch gate. |

## Retainer work

| Line item | Purpose | Typical deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly Governance Review | Review AI outputs, chatbot logs and source updates. | Monthly review notes and action list. |
| Content Performance Review | Review analytics, search, FAQs and content gaps. | Monthly/quarterly improvement plan. |
| Chatbot Improvement Cycle | Review transcripts, failures and new intents. | Updated tests, sources and prompt rules. |
