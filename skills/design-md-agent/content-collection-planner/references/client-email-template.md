```text
Subject: Content and source material needed for your website and AI readiness work

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the discovery input so far. The next step is to gather the source material we need to plan the website content, governance rules and any future AI-assisted or chatbot workflows properly.

Please use the attached checklist to share existing documents, links or files where possible. You do not need to rewrite everything from scratch. Existing approved material is usually more useful than polished new copy at this stage.

The most important items are:

1. Current business, product or service information
2. Brand assets and tone-of-voice examples
3. Key website page content or source notes
4. FAQs and repeated customer questions
5. Policies, terms, privacy or compliance documents
6. Any documents that should or should not be used as source material for AI/chatbot work

Where something is missing, please mark it as missing rather than delaying the whole process. We will use the gaps to plan the right next steps.

Important note: please do not share sensitive personal information, confidential customer records or regulated information unless we have agreed a secure process for handling it.

Best,
[Name]
```
