## Purpose

Confirm that major Figma sections exist as WordPress patterns or template parts and can be reused consistently by editors.

## Check

- hero sections
- service cards
- solution cards
- proof/stat blocks
- CTA sections
- FAQ sections
- testimonial sections
- case-study cards
- navigation and footer
- form sections

## Pattern quality questions

1. Is the pattern reusable?
2. Does it preserve design-system spacing and typography?
3. Does it work in light and dark mode?
4. Does it work on mobile?
5. Can editors safely modify content without breaking layout?
6. Are required fields documented?
