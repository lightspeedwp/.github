# Escalation and Unsupported Question Rules

## Escalate to human when users ask about

- fixed pricing or final quotes
- project timelines or availability
- legal, privacy or compliance advice
- security incidents, hacked sites or urgent technical issues
- account-specific support
- confidential client details
- personal data handling beyond approved policy wording
- unsupported AI, ROI, traffic, ranking or performance guarantees
- complaints
- employment/HR matters
