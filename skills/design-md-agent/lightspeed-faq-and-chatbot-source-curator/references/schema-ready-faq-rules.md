# Schema-Ready FAQ Rules

## Use FAQPage schema only when

- the question and answer are visible on the page
- the answer is public-facing
- the answer is complete and helpful
- the answer does not contain hidden promotional content
- the content is not misleading
- the page is not using FAQ schema for content users cannot see

## Answer style

Schema-ready answers should:

- answer directly
- stay concise
- avoid unsupported claims
- avoid multiple unrelated questions in one answer
- avoid legal/privacy/compliance advice unless approved policy wording exists
- avoid pricing promises unless approved
