# Privacy and Consent Notes

This skill supports operational tracking design and is not legal advice.

## Questions to answer

1. Which analytics tools are used?
2. Is GTM installed?
3. Is cookie consent required for analytics or marketing tags?
4. Are advertising pixels or remarketing tags used?
5. Where do form submissions go?
6. Is personal data sent to third-party tools?
7. Are chatbot transcripts stored later?
8. Who owns privacy review?
9. What does the privacy policy say about analytics and forms?
10. What does the cookie policy say about analytics cookies?

## Data minimisation rule

Do not send personally identifiable information to GA4 event names or parameters.
