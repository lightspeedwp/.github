# Measurement Workflow

## Sequence

1. Confirm business goals.
2. List conversion points.
3. Map pages to CTAs and forms.
4. Define GA4 events and parameters.
5. Define GTM triggers.
6. Confirm privacy and consent constraints.
7. Create QA checklist.
8. Create launch baseline report.
9. Define post-launch review cadence.

## Business goal categories

- qualified leads
- consultation bookings
- lead magnet downloads
- service-page engagement
- solution-page routing
- content engagement
- chatbot handoffs later
- ecommerce events if relevant

## Output stance

Use tables. Mark items as:

- Required before launch
- Recommended before launch
- Phase 2
- Pending decision
