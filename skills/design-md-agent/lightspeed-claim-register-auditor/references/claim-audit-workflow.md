# Claim Audit Workflow

1. Extract all explicit claims.
2. Extract implied claims.
3. Group duplicates.
4. Assign claim type.
5. Identify page location or intended use.
6. Check source evidence.
7. Classify with the standard labels.
8. Draft approved or safer wording.
9. List rejected wording.
10. List evidence needed.
11. Assign owner and review date.
12. Mark chatbot suitability.
13. Summarise publication risk.

## Duplicates

When the same claim appears in multiple locations, keep one master claim ID and list all page locations.

## Review cycle

For website launch, review high-risk claims before content freeze. For live sites, review claim registers quarterly or whenever claims change.
