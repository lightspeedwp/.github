Use these categories when auditing claims.

| Claim type | Examples | Risk level |
|---|---|---|
| Experience claim | years in business, project counts, sites hosted | Low to medium |
| Delivery claim | response times, process stages, launch quality | Medium |
| Performance claim | Core Web Vitals, Lighthouse, LCP, speed gains | Medium to high |
| SEO/search claim | rankings, traffic growth, snippets, AI citations | High |
| AI/automation claim | prediction accuracy, chatbot resolution, cost reduction | High |
| Accessibility claim | WCAG compliance, accessible-by-default, AAA | High |
| Privacy/compliance claim | GDPR, POPIA, data sovereignty, no training | High |
| Security claim | protected, secured, zero data loss, hardened | High |
| Case-study outcome | client growth, efficiency gains, migration success | Medium to high |
| Commercial claim | ROI, cost savings, conversion lift, lead quality | High |
| Policy/trust claim | governance, human review, disclosure, retention | Medium to high |

Flag implied claims too. For example, "AI-ready" implies governance, sources, privacy, testing and measurement.
