# Chatbot Claim Rules

A claim is chatbot-safe only when it is:

- public
- approved
- current
- evidence-backed
- included in an approved website page, FAQ, policy or case study
- not legal, privacy, compliance or security advice
- not a guarantee of ranking, revenue, ROI, uptime, accuracy or resolution

## Chatbot-safe statuses

Use:

- `Chatbot safe`
- `Chatbot safe after review`
- `Not for chatbot`
- `Legal/privacy review before chatbot`

Raw stats files, internal strategy notes, draft claims and unverified AI/ROI claims are not chatbot-safe.
