# Internal Linking Rules

## Purpose

Use internal links to clarify relationships between services, solutions, proof, governance and conversion routes.

## Recommended linking patterns

| From | Link to | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Core services, solutions, work, AI readiness checklist | Route users quickly. |
| Services overview | Individual service pages | Support service discovery. |
| Solution pages | Relevant service pages and case studies | Connect audience need to delivery capability. |
| Service pages | Related services, FAQs, case studies, consultation | Support conversion and depth. |
| AI pages | AI Governance, Privacy, FAQ, consultation | Build trust and reduce overclaiming. |
| Case studies | Related services and solutions | Turn proof into next action. |
| FAQ page | Relevant service and policy pages | Help users continue. |
| Blog/articles | Services, solutions, guides, lead magnets | Support topic authority. |

## Anchor text guidance

Prefer descriptive anchors such as:

- WordPress redesign services
- WooCommerce performance support
- AI readiness audit
- publishing workflow solutions
- Figma-to-WordPress design systems

Avoid vague anchors such as:

- click here
- learn more
- this page
