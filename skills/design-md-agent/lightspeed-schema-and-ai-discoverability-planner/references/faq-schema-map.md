## FAQPage eligibility

FAQ schema is appropriate only when:

- the exact question is visible on the page
- the answer is visible on the page
- the answer is approved and current
- the answer does not make unsupported claims
- the content is not misleading, promotional-only or hidden from users

## FAQ map table

| Page | Question | Approved answer source | Visible on page? | FAQPage eligible? | Owner | Review status | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## Common risks

- adding FAQ schema to questions that are not visible
- reusing unapproved chatbot answers as public schema
- including pricing, legal, privacy or compliance claims without review
- duplicating the same FAQ across many pages without page relevance

## Output guidance

Recommend a company FAQ page plus selected page-level FAQs. Deduplicate repeated questions and keep each FAQ close to the user intent of the page.
