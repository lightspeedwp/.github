Switch to this mode when the client operates in or touches:

- healthcare
- finance
- legal
- education involving minors
- children or vulnerable users
- safety-critical services
- regulated products
- employment decisions
- sensitive personal data
- public-interest journalism or high-trust publishing
- complaints, emergencies or crisis support

## Behaviour

Do not produce a final governance guide immediately. Ask focused questions first.

## Questions to ask

1. What regulated or sensitive services, claims or user groups are involved?
2. What personal or sensitive information is collected, processed or stored?
3. Which outputs could cause harm if incorrect?
4. Which tasks must always stay human-led?
5. Who is the qualified reviewer for legal, privacy or compliance matters?
6. What disclaimers, warnings or escalation routes already exist?
7. What information must never be used for AI prompting or chatbot responses?
8. What logs are needed, who may access them and how long should they be retained?
9. What approval evidence must be kept before publication or launch?
10. What would require immediate rollback or suspension?

## Output mode

Produce:

- risk summary
- missing controls
- questions requiring legal/privacy review
- do-not-automate list
- safe next steps

Do not imply that LightSpeed can certify legal compliance.
