# Content Collection And Drafting Rules

## When Content Is Not Ready

Produce:

- a content gap report
- a source-of-truth register recommendation
- approval and suitability classification
- a collection-first recommendation when gaps are material

## When AI-Assisted Drafting Is Allowed

Apply all of these rules:

- use only approved source material
- label all AI-created content as draft
- require human review before publication
- require human review before chatbot grounding
- do not let drafts become approved sources automatically
- distinguish between content safe for website drafting, content safe for chatbot grounding, and content needing legal or policy review

## Hard Rule

No generated content becomes a chatbot source until it has passed the approval route defined in governance.
