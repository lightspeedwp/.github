# Content Strategy Rules

Use this checklist to judge content readiness.

## Questions

- What content currently exists?
- Which content is explicitly approved for chatbot grounding?
- Which content is outdated, weak, partial, or unsupported?
- Are FAQs available?
- Are policies available?
- Are service, product, or support pages strong enough?
- Are structured fields, product data, or custom fields important?
- Is the use case simple enough for bounded answers from approved pages?
- Is retrieval genuinely needed, or is it being over-specified?
- Does the project need a source register before chatbot scoping?

## Rules

- Prefer bounded, approved page content before adding retrieval complexity.
- Recommend embeddings or advanced knowledge workflows only when the content shape genuinely requires them.
- If source material is weak, contradictory, or unapproved, route to source curation or content collection first.
