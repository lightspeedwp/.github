# Source Approval Rules

Classify each source or content item as one of:

- Approved
- Unconfirmed
- Excluded
- Missing
- Needs Rewrite
- Legal Review

## Rules

- Do not treat content as approved just because it exists.
- Separate chatbot-safe source material from general website content.
- Preserve explicit exclusions.
- If approval is unclear, default to Unconfirmed.
- Mark policy-sensitive or legally sensitive material as Legal Review when appropriate.
