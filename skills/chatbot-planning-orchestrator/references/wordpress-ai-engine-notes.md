# WordPress And AI Engine Notes

Use this reference only after the planning, approval, and governance gates are already clear.

## Positioning Rules

- Treat WordPress and AI Engine notes as implementation context, not as the reason to approve chatbot scope.
- Do not treat plugin capability as justification for governance or source shortcuts.
- Treat uploads, transcripts, retention, callable actions, and integrations as governance decisions first.
- Do not recommend advanced automation, function calling, MCP, or live web search as standard scope.
- Keep bounded chatbot behaviour ahead of platform flexibility.
