Use this example for competitions, giveaways, or campaign entries where consent, eligibility, marketing opt-in, and entry retention need careful handling.

## Recommended stance

- Separate competition entry consent from marketing opt-in.
- Do not assume eligibility, winner selection, prize terms, or legal wording is approved.
- Avoid collecting unnecessary personal data.
- Do not connect marketing feeds unless opt-in consent is explicit and the integration is available.

## Suggested fields

1. Name
2. Email
3. Phone optional
4. Region/location if needed for eligibility
5. Answer/entry field
6. Terms/competition consent
7. Optional marketing opt-in

## Confirmation

Acknowledge receipt without declaring eligibility or winner status.

## Notifications

Route admin entries to the campaign owner. Include entry source and opt-in state.

## Test cases

- Submit with and without optional marketing consent.
- Confirm entries record required consent.
- Confirm marketing feed does not run without opt-in.
- Confirm retention and export/erase stance is documented.
