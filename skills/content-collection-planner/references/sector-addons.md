## WooCommerce / ecommerce

Add:

- product categories
- product descriptions
- variation data
- product images
- stock/availability rules
- shipping zones and delivery rules
- returns/refunds policy
- payment methods
- product recommendation rules
- review policy
- transactional email content
- customer support FAQs
- abandoned cart/email flows if relevant

## Tour operator / tourism

Add:

- destinations
- itineraries
- accommodation details
- activity descriptions
- seasonality
- inclusions/exclusions
- departure dates or availability language
- booking terms
- cancellation rules
- traveller FAQs
- safety/travel notes
- Wetu or itinerary source links if relevant

## Publisher / news / media

Add:

- publication structure
- editorial categories
- author/editor roles
- syndication rules
- sponsored content policy
- advertorial rules
- taxonomy plan
- ad inventory notes
- editorial workflow
- correction policy
- archive/content migration notes

## Education / LMS

Add:

- courses
- modules
- lessons
- outcomes
- prerequisites
- access rules
- user roles
- support paths
- onboarding emails
- certificates/accreditation
- billing/subscription rules
- student FAQs

## Professional services

Add:

- service methodology
- consultation flow
- qualifications
- team credentials
- case studies
- proof of outcomes
- client onboarding content
- compliance claims
- proposal expectations
- common objections

## Memberships and subscriptions

Add:

- membership tiers
- access rules
- renewal rules
- cancellation policy
- billing FAQs
- onboarding flow
- member-only content
- account support
- upgrade/downgrade logic
- retention emails

## Regulated or sensitive sectors

Add stricter controls:

- evidence for all claims
- legal/compliance reviewer
- disclaimer requirements
- no-go topics
- escalation rules
- source review frequency
- incident response contacts
- chatbot exclusion rules
