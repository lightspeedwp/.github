## Core rule

If a claim is not approved and evidenced, do not state it as fact.

## Avoid

- guaranteed AI citations
- guaranteed rankings
- guaranteed featured snippets
- guaranteed ROI
- fully compliant
- 100% brand accuracy
- 100% schema compliance
- zero risk
- 400% more snippets
- 250% AI citation increase
- fixed chatbot resolution rates without project data

## Prefer

| Risky wording | Safer wording |
|---|---|
| Guaranteed AI citations | Improve the signals AI systems can interpret and cite. |
| 250% increase in AI citations | Track AI citation visibility before and after structured content improvements. |
| 400% more featured snippets | Improve eligibility for answer-led search experiences. |
| Fully GDPR compliant | Designed to support privacy-aware workflows, subject to legal/privacy review. |
| 100% schema compliance | Implement and validate structured data against current requirements. |
| 89% chatbot resolution | Set a measured pilot target once approved sources and escalation rules are in place. |
| 50% AI cost reduction | Optimise AI costs through caching, rate limits, model choice and monitoring. |

## Output note

When rewriting claims, include the original claim, suggested wording and evidence needed.
