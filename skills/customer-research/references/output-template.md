Use this structure unless the user asks for a different format.

## Customer / prospect

- **Name:**
- **Website / domain:**
- **Primary contact(s):**
- **Known relationship owner:**

## Research scope

- **Mode:** pre-call brief | outreach prep | proposal context | follow-up context | account history | renewal or upsell prep | reactivation | handoff
- **Window:** use absolute dates
- **Assumed scope:** state any interpretation made from incomplete input

## 3-bullet sales summary

- **Value:** the strongest confirmed commercial or relationship opportunity.
- **Risk:** the most important blocker, trust issue, or evidence gap.
- **Next step:** the smallest useful action for the sales assistant or salesperson.

## Relationship context

Summarise recent interactions, last touch, commitments, proposal status, active work, support context, and stakeholder notes. Cite every material claim.

## Opportunity and fit signals

List practical signals that support outreach, discovery, proposal progression, renewal, or upsell. Keep this evidence-led; do not score the account unless the user supplied a scoring model.

## Risks, blockers, or reasons to slow down

Call out unresolved support issues, delivery risk, unclear ownership, pricing concerns, stale evidence, stakeholder ambiguity, or missing approval signals.

## Recommended next action

Give one primary action and, where useful, one fallback.

## Suggested follow-up angle

Provide a brief internal angle or talking point. Do not write a full customer-facing email unless requested.

## Open questions

List only questions that would change the sales action, scope, or risk posture.

## Source coverage

Use a compact ledger:

| Handle | Source | Coverage | Supports | Freshness |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [G1] | Gmail thread with contact | relevant | last touch, commitments | 2026-06-24 |
| [W1] | Company website | relevant | positioning, services | checked 2026-06-25 |
| [D1] | Proposal doc | unavailable | proposal status | not accessible |

Coverage values: relevant, no result, not relevant, unavailable, intentionally skipped.

## Confidence

Use one of:

- **High:** recent direct evidence supports the relationship context and recommendation.
- **Medium:** direction is clear, but some sources are missing, stale, or indirect.
- **Low:** target, current state, or next step is unclear because evidence is thin or contradictory.
