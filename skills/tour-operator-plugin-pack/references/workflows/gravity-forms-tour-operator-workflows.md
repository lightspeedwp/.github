Gravity Forms supports the enquiry workflow; it does not replace the Tour Operator content model.

## Audit checklist

1. Identify all forms used by tour, destination, accommodation and general enquiry CTAs.
2. Confirm form fields, required states and accessible labels.
3. Confirm hidden/context fields capture tour, destination, accommodation, URL or referrer where needed.
4. Confirm notifications, conditional routing and recipients.
5. Confirm confirmations and user-facing success/failure wording.
6. Confirm anti-spam controls.
7. Confirm consent wording and storage.
8. Confirm operational handoff: owner, inbox/CRM, response SLA and missed-lead detection.
9. Confirm recent successful entries or test submissions where access allows.
10. Report missed-lead risk clearly.

## Implementation notes

Treat routing, consent and production notification changes as medium risk unless the site has a tested rollback and clear owner approval.
