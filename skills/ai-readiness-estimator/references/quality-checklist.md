## Routing quality

- The output starts from the requested outcome, not a skill name.
- The main workflow is used when no specialist skill is a strong match.
- The selected specialist skill is the narrowest suitable option.
- Onboarding is used only for durable reusable defaults.
- Drafting skills are not used before source material is approved.
- Audit or validation skills are reserved for review stages.

## Evidence quality

- Evidence reviewed is listed or summarised.
- Confirmed facts are separated from assumptions.
- Missing, stale, contradictory, or weak evidence is labelled.
- Source-of-truth gaps are called out before scope or implementation commitments.
- Chatbot recommendations are not made as implementation-ready unless source content, governance, privacy, and escalation paths are adequate.

## Commercial quality

- Base package or base workstream is chosen before add-ons.
- Pricing, timeline, approval, and scope confidence are caveated where incomplete.
- Implementation-heavy work is not treated as included unless explicitly confirmed.
- Follow-on scope and exclusions are clear.

## Team usability

- Output is scannable and copy-ready.
- Internal LightSpeed notes are separated from client-facing wording.
- Next action is explicit.
- No local-only file paths or inaccessible links are exposed.
- Human review gates are included for legal, privacy, security, claims, and publication-sensitive work.
