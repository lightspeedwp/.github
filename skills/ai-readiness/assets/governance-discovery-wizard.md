```text
Use the ai-governance-documentor workflow.

Run a guided AI governance discovery process for a client. Ask one section at a time.

Collect enough information to produce a governance discovery summary and, if possible, a draft governance guide.

Sections:
1. Business overview
2. Website and digital ecosystem
3. Audience and customer journey
4. Brand and positioning
5. Voice and tone
6. Visual style and reusable design choices
7. Content inventory and ownership
8. AI goals, use cases and boundaries
9. Risk, privacy and compliance context
10. Chatbot goals and website role
11. Project priorities and deliverables

If regulated, sensitive, children, health, finance, legal, safety-critical or high-reputation-risk context appears, switch to stricter interactive mode and ask more detailed risk questions before drafting outputs.

At the end, produce:
- Workshop-style discovery summary
- Minimum missing inputs
- Approved AI use cases
- Excluded AI use cases
- Human-only tasks
- Suggested role map
- Early governance matrix
- Recommended next step
- Internal LightSpeed notes

Use UK English and include the standard legal/privacy disclaimer.
```
