## Purpose

Use `ai-governance-documentor` to turn discovery answers, risk context, business notes and website evidence into AI governance outputs.

## Core outputs

- governance discovery summary
- lightweight AI policy
- operational AI governance guide
- governance matrix
- source-of-truth register
- role map
- approved prompt templates
- chatbot system instruction templates
- human review and approval rules
- incident and review workflow

## Workflow

Combine discovery and final documentation.

Use the discovery process first. Then produce the final governance documentation based on the answers.

## Minimum recommended inputs

Before generating a full governance guide, collect:

- client name
- website URL
- sector
- project stage
- business goals
- approved AI use cases
- excluded AI use cases
- sensitive data context
- legal/privacy context
- content owners
- approvers
- source-of-truth materials
- chatbot intent, if relevant
- review frequency
- incident contact

## Output style by deliverable

Use different tones depending on the output:

- Policy outputs: formal policy style
- Operational governance guides: practical playbook style
- Reports and assessment summaries: workshop summary style

## Required governance roles

Define these roles where relevant:

- Governance Owner
- Content Owner
- Content Approver
- Legal/Compliance Approver
- Chatbot Owner
- Source Material Owner
- Incident Contact
- Technical Owner
- Analytics/Reporting Owner

Group roles under umbrella responsibilities where useful:

- Strategic Ownership
- Content and Brand Ownership
- Risk and Compliance Ownership
- Technical Ownership
- Continuous Improvement Ownership

## Required governance matrix columns

- AI activity
- Owner
- Risk level
- Source material
- Review required?
- Approval required?
- Allowed tools
- Prohibited tools
- Review frequency
- Notes

## Framework references

Reference these where useful, without overloading the client:

- NIST AI Risk Management Framework
- ISO/IEC 42001
- EU AI Act risk-based and transparency approach
- UK ICO AI and data protection guidance
- POPIA principles where relevant
- OWASP Top 10 for LLM Applications
- WCAG 2.2
- Google Search guidance on useful AI-assisted content
- WordPress governance, performance and security best practice
