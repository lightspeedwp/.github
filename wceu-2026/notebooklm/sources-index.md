## HOW TO USE THIS INDEX IN NOTEBOOKLM

1. **Create new NotebookLM session** at <https://www.notebooklm.google.com>
2. **Paste all URLs above** into the "Add sources" field (one per line)
3. **Add main brief**: Copy entire contents of `wceu-2026/SLIDES_GENERATION_PROMPT.md` and paste as the primary document
4. **Ask NotebookLM**:
   - "Generate detailed speaker notes and talking points for each of the 24 slides in this talk"
   - "Provide visual suggestions (diagrams, flowcharts, before/after comparisons) for each slide"
   - "Identify key metrics and examples to include in the talk"
   - "Suggest concrete examples from the LightSpeed repository that illustrate each concept"
5. **Export output** as text and use as briefs for transferring to Google Slides
