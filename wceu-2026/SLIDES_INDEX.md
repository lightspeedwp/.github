## Questions & Notes

**Q: Should I add animations?**
A: Keep animations minimal. Slide transitions only, no text animations. Avoid motion that could trigger photosensitivity.

**Q: Can I add videos?**
A: If adding video (max 30 sec), caption it. Keep total video under 2 minutes across entire presentation.

**Q: How do I handle Q&A?**
A: Reserve 3–5 minutes after final slide. Repeat questions for audience. Reference specific slides if needed.

**Q: What if I run over time?**
A: Priority order for cuts: Slide 7 (WordPress alignment), Slide 20 (customization), Slide 21 (standards). Never cut Slides 1–6 (hook) or Slides 22–24 (ROI + principle).

**Q: Dark theme too dark for room?**
A: Test projector brightness. If needed, increase text size slightly (44pt minimum body, 56pt minimum titles).
