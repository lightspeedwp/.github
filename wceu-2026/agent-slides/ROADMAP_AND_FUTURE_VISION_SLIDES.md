# Roadmap & Future Vision Slide Deck Prompt

## System Overview

The **Roadmap & Future Vision System** outlines the strategic direction of the .github automation ecosystem, planned capabilities, investment areas, and long-term vision. It communicates where the system is heading and what to expect in upcoming releases.

**Operational scope**: Strategic planning, feature roadmap, capability expansion, version planning, vision communication.

**Owned by**: LightSpeed leadership & product teams

## Key Strategic Pillars

1. **Automation Excellence** - Increasing coverage of automated workflows
2. **Developer Experience** - Making automation accessible and intuitive
3. **Ecosystem Integration** - Connecting with tools, services, and platforms
4. **Observability & Insights** - Better visibility into automation health
5. **Scalability & Performance** - Supporting larger teams and repositories
6. **Plugin Extensibility** - Making it easy to add custom capabilities

## Timeline Perspectives

- **Current State** (Now): 7 agents, 5 plugins, 15+ workflows, comprehensive automation
- **Short Term** (Q3 2026): Enhanced observability, improved DX, new WordPress plugins
- **Medium Term** (H2 2026 - H1 2027): Advanced analytics, multi-repo coordination, AI-powered suggestions
- **Long Term** (2027+): Fully autonomous operations, predictive automation, ecosystem-wide insights

## Use Cases & Examples

### Use Case 1: Quarterly Planning Session

Team planning for Q3 2026; using roadmap to align on priorities.

**Planning flow:**

1. Review current state: What's working, what's not?
2. Assess roadmap items: What's critical vs. nice-to-have?
3. Customer feedback: What are teams asking for?
4. Resource planning: What can we realistically deliver?
5. Prioritization: Rank roadmap items
6. Commitment: Team agrees on Q3 goals
7. Execution: Epics created, sprints planned

### Use Case 2: Communicating Vision to Stakeholders

Leadership meeting; CEO asks "Where is .github going?"

**Communication flow:**

1. Show vision: Where will .github be in 2 years?
2. Strategic pillars: How are we getting there?
3. Current progress: How far along are we?
4. Planned milestones: What's next?
5. Investment required: What resources needed?
6. Expected impact: What will this enable?
7. Timeline: When can we expect what?

### Use Case 3: Requesting New Plugin

Team wants WordPress community management plugin; where does it fit?

**Planning flow:**

1. Request: "We need automated community management"
2. Evaluate roadmap: Does this fit strategic pillars?
3. Assessment: Aligns with "Ecosystem Integration"
4. Planning: When could we tackle this?
5. Scope: What would plugin include?
6. Resources: Who would implement?
7. Timeline: Q4 2026 target identified

## Slide Structure (12-15 slides)

**Slide 01** - Hook & Stakes

- Vision: Automation becomes increasingly intelligent and autonomous
- Stakes: If we don't plan strategically, we become reactive; miss opportunities

**Slide 02** - Vision Statement

- **2-year vision**: The most trusted, extensible automation ecosystem for WordPress projects
- **Goals**:
  - 95% of routine operations are automated
  - Developers can extend with custom plugins without limits
  - Real-time visibility into all automation activities
  - Predictive insights guide decisions
  - Multi-repo coordination seamless

**Slide 03** - Strategic Pillars

1. **Automation Excellence**: Expand from 15 to 25+ workflows; cover edge cases; improve reliability
2. **Developer Experience**: Better documentation, easier customization, clearer feedback
3. **Ecosystem Integration**: Connect with more tools: Jira, Slack, Analytics, etc.
4. **Observability & Insights**: Richer metrics, predictive alerts, business intelligence
5. **Scalability & Performance**: Support teams of 100+ developers, 10+ repositories
6. **Plugin Extensibility**: Plugin marketplace, plugin discovery, shared templates

**Slide 04** - Current State (May 2026)

- **Agents**: 7 autonomous decision-makers (release, branding, meta, reviewer, linting, labelling, planner)
- **Plugins**: 5 domain-specific plugins (github-ops, release-ops, wordpress-governance, wordpress-planning, metrics-and-reporting)
- **Skills**: 30+ reusable capabilities distributed across 6 batches
- **Workflows**: 15+ GitHub Actions workflows covering major processes
- **Quality**: Comprehensive testing, accessibility compliance, security scanning
- **Observability**: Session logging, audit trails, metrics dashboard
- **Adoption**: Used by LightSpeed and partner teams

**Slide 05** - Q3 2026 Goals (3-4 months)

- **Skill expansion**: Add 8 new skills (WordPress governance, advanced metrics)
- **Observability enhancements**: Real-time dashboard, anomaly detection
- **DX improvements**: Video tutorials, interactive guides, troubleshooting bot
- **Performance**: Reduce average workflow time by 20%
- **Community**: First external plugin contribution
- **Metrics**: 99.5% automation success rate

**Slide 06** - H2 2026 Goals (6 months)

- **Advanced analytics**: Predictive metrics, trend forecasting, capacity planning
- **AI integration**: Copilot-assisted automation, suggestion engine
- **Ecosystem expansion**: 3 new plugins, integration with Jira and Slack

**Slide 07** - H1 2027 Goals (12 months)

- **Autonomous operations**: Minimal human intervention required
- **Predictive intelligence**: Anticipate issues before they occur
- **Self-healing**: Detect and remediate problems automatically
- **Custom agents**: Teams can define custom decision-makers
- **Integration hub**: Connect with 20+ external tools and services

**Slide 08** - Feature Roadmap: Near Term (Q3-Q4 2026)

**Slide 09** - Feature Roadmap: Medium Term (2027)

- **Capacity planning AI**: Recommend team size and structure

**Slide 10** - Plugin Ecosystem Expansion

- **Q3 2026**: 2 new plugins (wordpress-blocks-validation, wordpress-community-moderation)

- `.github/VISION.md` - Vision statement (if exists)

- Project board "Future" - Backlog of ideas

## Design Notes

- **Visual theme**: Future-focused vision (growth, trajectory, expansion arrows, timeline)
- **Color palette**: Use forward-looking colors (blues, purples, progressive greens)
- **Key visuals**: Timeline diagram, roadmap visualization, strategic pillar connections, capability expansion chart
- **Accessibility**: Clear timeline labels, high contrast for progress indicators
- **Animations**: Consider timeline reveal, growth animation, milestone celebration

## Quality Bar

- Ground vision in realistic assessment of current state
- Validate roadmap items against team capacity
- Show progression from current to future
- Include real timeline and milestones
- Connect vision to business outcomes
- Show evidence of progress toward goals
- Ensure all references point to current develop branch
