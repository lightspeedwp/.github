## Goal

Generate personalized, professional cold outreach emails that achieve high open and response rates while building genuine business relationships.

## Inputs

Before using this template, provide the following information:

- **Prospect Name**: Full name of the person you're contacting
- **Company Name**: Name of their company/organization
- **Company Industry**: Their business sector or industry
- **Prospect Role**: Their job title or position
- **Pain Point**: Specific challenge their industry/role typically faces
- **Your Solution**: How your product/service addresses their pain point
- **Call to Action**: What you want them to do (demo, call, meeting, etc.)
- **Your Name**: Your full name
- **Company Name**: Your company name
- **Credentials**: Brief credibility marker (years of experience, notable clients, etc.)

## Instructions

1. Start with a personalized subject line that mentions their company or industry
2. Open with a specific observation about their company, recent news, or industry trend
3. Acknowledge their time constraints and be direct about your purpose
4. Present one specific pain point relevant to their role/industry
5. Offer a concrete solution with a brief credibility statement
6. Include a soft, low-commitment call to action
7. Keep the total email under 150 words
8. End with a professional signature

## Quality Checks

The generated email should meet these criteria:

- [ ] Subject line is specific and personalized (not generic)
- [ ] Opening demonstrates research about their company/industry
- [ ] Value proposition is clear and relevant to their role
- [ ] Tone is professional but conversational
- [ ] Email length is 100-150 words maximum
- [ ] Contains one clear, low-pressure call to action
- [ ] No spelling or grammatical errors
- [ ] Professional email signature included

## Safety & Limits

**Do not use this template for:**

- Mass email campaigns without personalization
- Misleading or false claims about your product/service
- Aggressive or pushy sales tactics
- Contacting people who have opted out of communications
- Industries you don't understand or can't help

**Always ensure:**

- Compliance with CAN-SPAM Act and GDPR
- Truthful representation of your offerings
- Respect for the recipient's time and inbox
- Follow-up limits (maximum 2-3 follow-ups)

## Examples

### Example Input

- **Prospect Name**: Sarah Chen
- **Company Name**: TechStart Solutions
- **Company Industry**: SaaS startup
- **Prospect Role**: CTO
- **Pain Point**: Scaling development team efficiently
- **Your Solution**: Staff augmentation with vetted developers
- **Call to Action**: 15-minute call to discuss their hiring challenges
- **Your Name**: Alex Rodriguez
- **Company Name**: DevScale Partners
- **Credentials**: 8 years helping startups scale development teams
